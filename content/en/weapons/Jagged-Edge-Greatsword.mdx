The **Jagged Edge greatsword** in Phantom Blade Zero is one of the most punishing Strength-scaling weapons players can wield, trading fluid combos for raw, serrated damage that punishes anything caught flat-footed. With its bleed-infused blade and stagger-heavy swings, the weapon rewards timing over spamming, making it a favorite among veterans chasing peak damage windows. ## Jagged Edge Greatsword Overview

The Jagged Edge greatsword is a two-handed, Strength-leaning weapon built around three pillars: massive single-hit damage, mid-combo stagger pressure, and a built-in bleed proc that scales with hit count rather than raw Dexterity. Players who prefer the rapid poke-and-roll style of the **Hollow Needles** sword line will find this blade's deliberate cadence unfamiliar, but those who commit to its rhythm quickly discover why high-tier community builds overwhelmingly slot it into Breached-state punish windows.

The Phantom Blade Zero Jagged Edge greatsword caps its Physical Attack ceiling above most mid-tier katanas once Strength passes 30, which is why most veteran setups push their primary stat past 40 before investing elsewhere. The dual Strength/Dexterity bleed scaling looks tempting, but community testing shows the Strength route pulls roughly 18–22% more total damage on a fully charged follow-up.

### Weapon Archetype and Playstyle

This greatsword belongs to the **Heavy Slam** archetype, meaning every swing carries a brief recovery window you cannot cancel. In exchange, the hitboxes are forgiving, the tip-tracking is generous against retreating foes, and a fully landed three-hit chain can crack an enemy's Breached posture gauge before the animation finishes. If you're new to the game's timing windows, the [Phantom Blade Zero Combat Guide](/en/guides/combat-guide/) walks through the dodge-and-punish loop that pairs perfectly with this blade.

## How to Unlock the Jagged Edge Greatsword

The Phantom Blade Zero Jagged Edge greatsword is not a starter weapon — it sits behind a mid-game crafting chain tied to a side region most players miss on their first playthrough. The unlock process is straightforward once you know where to look, but it requires two specific boss clears and a rare crafting reagent.

### Step-by-Step Unlock Path

| Step | Requirement | Location | Reward Component |
|------|-------------|----------|------------------|
| 1 | Defeat the **Serrated Warden** mini-boss | Forgotten Kiln dungeon | Jagged Edge Hilt |
| 2 | Clear the **Frostveil Chapel** side dungeon | Northern Snowpeak region | Jagged Edge Blade Shard |

The Rust-Hammered Smith only appears after you've spoken to the Wandering Quartermaster in the hub area at least once, so newcomers who rush past the merchant dialogue often miss the trigger. If you're still figuring out the early-game map, the [Phantom Blade Zero Beginner Guide](/en/guides/beginner-guide/) covers the hub NPC order you need to follow.

## Jagged Edge Greatsword Moveset & Combat Mechanics

Where most greatswords in Phantom Blade Zero trade speed for power, the Phantom Blade Zero Jagged Edge greatsword leans even further into the trade — its light chain is shorter, its recovery is longer, but every frame of that commitment pays you back with disproportionate stagger and bleed. Mastering the moveset is less about memorizing inputs and more about recognizing which attack the current fight rewards.

### Light Attack Chain Breakdown

The three-hit light chain is your bread and butter against standard enemies and trash mobs. It opens with a wide horizontal slash, follows with an overhead slam, and finishes with a thrust that pulls slightly forward to catch rolling targets. The first two hits have generous stagger windows that let you follow up immediately, but the third hit locks you into a longer recovery — never throw the full chain unless you've confirmed the enemy is staggered or Breached.

| Hit | Animation | Stagger | Bleed Buildup | Recovery |
|-----|-----------|---------|---------------|----------|
| Light 1 | Wide horizontal | Low (12) | 6 | 0.30s |
| Light 2 | Overhead slam | Medium (24) | 9 | 0.35s |
| Light 3 | Forward thrust | High (38) | 14 | 0.65s |

### Charged Heavy and Skill Art Integration

The charged heavy attack is where the Phantom Blade Zero Jagged Edge greatsword truly comes alive. Holding the heavy input for roughly one second unleashes a two-handed overhead smash that consumes 25 stamina but deals 2.1× your displayed Physical Attack and applies the weapon's signature **Serrated Wound** debuff — a 6-second bleed tick that ignores 30% of the target's Bleed resistance.

| Attack | Stamina | Damage Multiplier | Buff Applied | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------------------|--------------|-------|
| Charged Heavy | 25 | 2.1× | Serrated Wound | Best used after a dodge, not mid-chain |
| Skill Art: **Rending Cascade** | 60 | 3.4× | Serrated Wound + 2s posture stun | Combo finisher, only on staggered targets |
| Aerial Slam (jump + heavy) | 30 | 2.6× | Serrated Wound | Punishes enemies that whiff downward swings |

Pairing the charged heavy with proper dodge timing is essential. If your dodge timing is shaky, drilling it through the [Phantom Blade Zero How To Dodge](/en/guides/how-to-dodge/) tutorial loop will pay off within an hour of practice.

### Defensive Synergy: Sha-Chi and Ghostep

Because the greatsword's recovery is long, you cannot trade hits like a katana build. Instead, weave in **Sha-Chi** resource drains to create punish windows and use **Ghostep** to reposition behind an enemy mid-swing. Combining a well-timed Ghostep with the Skill Art **Rending Cascade** is the signature combo for any Phantom Blade Zero Jagged Edge greatsword player pushing into the late-game bosses. For a deeper dive on resource layering, the [Phantom Blade Zero Sha Chi Guide](/en/guides/sha-chi-guide/) breaks down when to spend and when to bank.

## Best Build & Stat Allocation for the Jagged Edge Greatsword

A weapon this stat-hungry punishes spread-thin allocations. The strongest Phantom Blade Zero Jagged Edge greatsword builds funnel roughly 80% of levels into Strength, with the remaining 20% split between Dexterity (for slightly faster cast times on Skill Arts) and Vigor (to survive the long recovery windows when mistimed).

### Recommended Stat Priority

| Stat | Early Game (Lv. 1–30) | Mid Game (Lv. 31–60) | End Game (Lv. 61+) |
|------|------------------------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Strength | 25 | 40 | 50 |
| Dexterity | 12 | 18 | 24 |
| Vigor | 15 | 25 | 35 |
| Endurance | 12 | 16 | 20 |
| Sha-Chi Affinity | 5 | 10 | 15 |

Players who skipped the Bleedstone farm and are running the weapon at Level 5 or below should prioritize Strength above all else, because the B-grade scaling won't activate until Strength crosses 25. As of patch 1.07, the B → S upgrade curve kicks in at Strength 30, which is the breakpoint most community build sheets target.

### Gem and Charm Synergies

The Phantom Blade Zero Jagged Edge greatsword accepts one weapon gem and one charm slot. The two highest-value options reported by the community are:

- **Serrated Gem** — adds 12 flat Bleed Buildup and extends Serrated Wound duration by 2 seconds.
- **Stoneheart Charm** — converts 8% of damage taken during a charged heavy wind-up into recoverable HP if the hit lands.

Slotting both together turns charged heavies into your highest sustain tool, which matters because the weapon cannot parry without a shield off-hand and you cannot dodge-cancel the wind-up. For fights where you need that extra defensive layer, the [Phantom Blade Zero How To Parry](/en/guides/how-to-parry/) guide covers off-hand shield pairings that complement this gem setup.

## Boss and Enemy Matchups for the Jagged Edge Greatsword

The Phantom Blade Zero Jagged Edge greatsword is not a universal answer — it dominates slow, large targets and struggles against fast swarms. Picking the right fights is half the battle, so this section breaks down where the weapon shines and where you should swap to a faster blade.

### Elite Enemy Breakdown

| Enemy Type | Matchup Rating | Recommended Approach |
|------------|----------------|----------------------|
| Heavy Knights | ★★★★★ | Full charged heavy after a parry |
| Beast-type Foes | ★★★★☆ | Light chain 1 → 2 → dodge repeat |
| Fast Humanoid Foes | ★★☆☆☆ | Swap to a katana for these encounters |
| Flying Enemies | ★★☆☆☆ | Aerial slam only, otherwise skip |
| Bleed-Immune Undead | ★★★☆☆ | Use raw Physical Attack, ignore Serrated Wound |

The heavy knight matchup is the most rewarding because their long telegraphed swings give you a free charged-heavy window every time. Conversely, fast humanoid enemies like the **Twin Fang Assassins** will shred your stamina before you can land a third light, so keep a secondary weapon for those rooms.

### Boss Phase Strategy

In multi-phase boss fights, the Phantom Blade Zero Jagged Edge greatsword's value peaks during the Breached state. Phase 1 should be conservative — bait one opening, land a single charged heavy, then back off to recover stamina. Phase 2 opens once the boss's posture gauge is broken, at which point you unload the full light chain into Skill Art **Rending Cascade** before resetting.

| Boss Phase | Priority Action | Stamina Budget | Risk Level |
|------------|------------------|----------------|------------|
| Phase 1 (Full HP) | Bait → one charged heavy | ≤ 40% | Low |
| Phase 2 (Breached) | Full chain → Rending Cascade | Spend all | High |
| Phase 3 (Enraged) | Punish whiffs only | ≤ 25% | Medium |

If your Breached-state execution is inconsistent, the [Phantom Blade Zero Breached State Recovery](/en/guides/breached-state-recovery/) tutorial explains the exact stamina breakpoints you need to hit to land the kill combo cleanly. Most failed runs trace back to entering Phase 2 with too little stamina banked, so treat the 40% threshold as a hard rule rather than a suggestion.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the Phantom Blade Zero Jagged Edge greatsword viable for new players?**

It can be, but it is a steep entry curve. The long recovery windows demand solid dodge timing, and the Strength-stat requirement delays its damage payoff until mid-game. New players are better served by a katana or spear until they've cleared at least one full dungeon. After that, the weapon becomes one of the strongest mid-to-late-game options in the entire armory.

**What's the best early stat investment for the Jagged Edge greatsword?**

Push Strength to 25 as soon as possible, then alternate between Vigor and Dexterity. The B-grade scaling doesn't activate until Strength 25, so anything below that feels sluggish and unrewarding. Once you cross 30 Strength, the damage jump is dramatic enough that you'll feel the difference immediately on the next dungeon clear.

**Can I parry with the Jagged Edge greatsword equipped?**

Not by default. Two-handed weapons disable the parry input unless you're holding a compatible off-hand shield. If parrying is essential for a specific boss, swap to a one-handed blade with a shield in the off-hand for that fight, then switch back to the Jagged Edge greatsword for the damage windows. This is the workaround most veteran players use when tackling parry-heavy encounters.

**Which charm works best with this greatsword?**

The **Stoneheart Charm** is the strongest pairing because it converts wind-up risk into sustain. Combined with the Serrated Gem, you can chain two charged heavies back-to-back and recover a meaningful HP chunk on each landed hit. Other charms are situationally useful, but none match the raw consistency of Stoneheart for solo boss progression.

**How does the Jagged Edge greatsword compare to other heavy weapons?**

Compared to the **Great Claymore** line, the Phantom Blade Zero Jagged Edge greatsword trades roughly 8% raw Physical Attack for significantly faster Bleed Buildup and a stronger Skill Art finisher. Against non-bleedable targets, the Claymore pulls ahead. Against anything vulnerable to Serrated Wound, the Jagged Edge greatsword wins on time-to-kill by a comfortable margin, which is why it remains a community favorite for boss-rush content.

Players who commit to learning its cadence find the Phantom Blade Zero Jagged Edge greatsword one of the most satisfying weapons in the entire roster — its weight feels earned, its damage feels decisive, and the Breached-state punish window is one of the cleanest in the game.
