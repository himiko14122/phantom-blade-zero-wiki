## Weapon Arsenal Overview in Phantom Blade Zero

The Phantom Blade Zero all weapons list spans **over 30 unique primary blades** and **more than 20 Phantom Edges**, giving players one of the deepest weapon rosters in the recent wuxia-action catalog. According to the [Phantom Blade Zero Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4115450/Phantom_Blade_Zero/) and the [official site description](https://pbz.s-game.com/), every blade belongs to one of five weapon families — longsword, greatsword, twin blades, chakram, and curved blades — each with its own stance, timing windows, and combo routes.

A primary blade defines your main **combo chain**, your dodge-cancel timings, and the speed at which your **Sha-Chi gauge** fills during combat. A Phantom Edge sits in the off-hand slot and activates on a separate button press, adding a layer of tactical depth that you will want to master early. If you are just starting out, the [Phantom Blade Zero beginner guide](/en/guides/beginner-guide/) walks through stance-switching and how weapon weight affects your recovery frames, which are crucial concepts before committing to a blade.

Below you will find every confirmed weapon category, the named blades within each family, and the role each plays in combat — based on official descriptions and community testing as of August 2026. S-Game has not yet released a full stats sheet, so percentages and stagger values may shift with future patches.

## Primary Blades by Weapon Family

Primary blades are the centerpiece of your build. Each family has its own attack speed, reach, and Sha-Chi scaling, so choosing a main blade is the single most important decision you make before a boss fight. The table below covers every named primary blade confirmed on the official store page and the supplementary report from [IGN's Phantom Blade Zero preview](https://www.ign.com/articles/phantom-blade-zero-is-a-delightfully-punishing-action-rpg).

|---|---|---|---|

| Longsword | Soft Snake Sword | Light | Aerial slash-cancel into dodge |
| Greatsword | Jagged Edge greatsword | Heavy | Stagger damage on overhead smashes |
| Greatsword | Juggernaut | Heavy | Highest single-hit damage in game |
| Twin Blades | White Serpent & Crimson Viper | Medium | Dual-element scaling, burn + bleed |
| Chakram | Seamless Death chakram | Light | Outbound + return projectile damage |

### Single-Hand Blades

Single-hand blades trade raw damage for the **fastest stamina recovery** in the game, which makes them ideal for players who rely on parrying and counter-attacks. The **Sanguine longsword** is the canonical starter weapon — a balanced blade with medium reach and clean hitboxes that is perfect for learning the parry windows covered in our [Phantom Blade Zero how to parry guide](/en/guides/how-to-parry/).

The **Soft Snake Sword** is the mobility-focused option in this family: light, curved, and tuned for slash-canceling into dodge. According to community testing, its third-light-attack chains into an aerial flip that completely resets enemy aggression timers for roughly one second. Because of that aerial flip, the Soft Snake Sword is also the easiest blade to combo into a **Ghostep** without dropping your damage window.

### Heavy Blades

Heavy blades hit like freight trains but punish mistimed dodges harshly because of their longer recovery frames. The **Jagged Edge greatsword** is the flagship heavy blade, with overhead smashes that can stagger most mid-tier bosses in two clean hits. Pairing it with a slow Phantom Edge gives you a deliberate, high-damage rhythm suited for players who prefer **tank-style combat strategies** in arena-fight boss rooms.

The **Juggernaut** — a massive two-handed blade confirmed on the official store page — is the slowest weapon in the roster. Its charged heavy attack deals enough **posture damage** to break a boss out of Breached State instantly, which is a critical tool for [Phantom Blade Zero Breached state recovery](/en/guides/breached-state-recovery/) fights where enemies re-enter aggressive phases after a failed stagger window.

### Twin Blade Sets

Twin blade sets sacrifice single-hit damage for absurdly fast combo chains. The **White Serpent & Crimson Viper** set is the marquee option — a left/right pairing where each blade has independent elemental scaling, letting you stack burn and bleed on the same enemy simultaneously. This makes it the strongest choice for damage-over-time builds against high-HP bosses, especially in the longer encounters that last more than three minutes.

Twin blade stamina costs are roughly 30 percent higher than single-hand blades per combo, so mastering the [Phantom Blade Zero Sha Chi guide](/en/guides/sha-chi-guide/) approach is essential to keep your **burst windows** fueled when running this family. The off-hand Crimson Viper also applies a small burn tick on every third hit, which compounds with the White Serpent's bleed stacks once both debuffs are active.

### Ranged Blades (Chakram)

Chakrams are the only ranged primary blade in the game, and the **Seamless Death chakram** is currently the only named option. It throws a spinning disc that returns to your hand, dealing damage on both the outbound and return pass — which roughly doubles its effective DPS compared to a standard projectile. The return arc can be aimed with a directional input, allowing you to curve the disc around pillars.

The trade-off is reach: chakram throws have a hard distance cap of roughly twelve meters, and the return arc can clip on level geometry. Players report that Seamless Death works best in arena-fight boss rooms where you control the spacing, as detailed in the broader [Phantom Blade Zero combat guide](/en/guides/combat-guide/).

## Phantom Edges: The Off-Hand Layer

Phantom Edges are secondary weapons that activate via a dedicated button, separate from your primary blade's combo chain. While you only equip one at a time, you can swap between two Phantom Edges at any **Meditation Point**, giving you flexible tactical coverage for every encounter. The official store page confirms more than 20 distinct Phantom Edges in the launch roster, with additional entries expected in post-launch patches.

### Light Phantom Edges

Light Phantom Edges are quick, utility-focused tools that interrupt enemy combos or apply status effects. They typically cost less than a quarter of your Sha-Chi gauge and recover almost instantly, which means you can weave them into nearly every encounter without disrupting your rhythm. Most of the **20+ Phantom Edges** in this tier fall into one of three roles: **parry extenders** that add extra deflect frames, **ranged interrupters** that stagger mid-combo enemies, and **status applicators** that apply poison, burn, or frost on activation.

Light Phantom Edges are also the safest way to extend a combo chain after a parry, because they fire during the brief invulnerability window of your counter stance. They are especially synergistic with single-hand blades like the Sanguine longsword, where the lower stamina cost leaves room for an extra edge activation per combo.

### Heavy Phantom Edges

Heavy Phantom Edges are activated finishers — slower than their light counterparts but capable of one-shotting weakened enemies or breaking boss posture outright. They are gated behind a longer cooldown and usually consume a third of your Sha-Chi gauge per use. According to community testing, heavy Phantom Edges are the most common tool used to extend combos after a successful **Ghostep execution**, because they fire during the brief invulnerability window that Ghostep grants.

The downside is that heavy Phantom Edges often leave you in a recovery animation for nearly a full second, which makes them risky against fast-attacking bosses. Pairing a heavy edge with a tank-style primary blade like the Juggernaut is the safest way to absorb that recovery window without losing positioning.

## Best Weapons by Playstyle

Choosing the right weapon is not about raw numbers — it is about how a blade's timing windows match your reflexes. Here are the three strongest picks per archetype based on community testing and the official weapon descriptions.

### Aggressive / DPS Builds

The **White Serpent & Crimson Viper** twin blade set is unmatched for sustained damage. Stack burn and bleed on the boss, then detonate with a heavy Phantom Edge when the enemy enters Breached State. Because twin blades drain stamina fast, pair them with deliberate **Sha-Chi management** so your burst windows stay fueled across long encounters. The Soft Snake Sword is the runner-up in this archetype for players who want mobility over raw sustained DPS.

### Defensive / Parry Builds

The **Sanguine longsword** remains the safest parry weapon because of its fast recovery frames and clean hitboxes. Combine it with a light Phantom Edge that extends your parry timing, and you will be able to deflect almost any attack in the game once you have mastered the timing windows. This build rewards players who can read telegraphs two to three seconds in advance, because every parry converts directly into a guaranteed counter-attack.

### Tank / Stagger Builds

The **Juggernaut** is the ultimate stagger tool. Its charged heavies break boss posture on the second hit, letting you control the pacing of every fight. Pair it with dodge-focused movement to offset its slow recovery — and remember that **posture damage** from Juggernaut stacks multiplicatively with Phantom Edge finishers, which can shorten boss fights by twenty to thirty seconds on average.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many weapons are in Phantom Blade Zero?**

There are **over 30 unique primary blades** and **more than 20 Phantom Edges** in Phantom Blade Zero, spread across five weapon families: longsword, greatsword, twin blades, chakram, and curved blades. The full Phantom Blade Zero all weapons list continues to grow with seasonal updates.

**What is the best starter weapon in Phantom Blade Zero?**

The **Sanguine longsword** is the recommended starter because it has the most forgiving recovery frames, making it the easiest blade on which to learn parry timings and dodge cancels. It scales well into the mid-game and remains viable in end-game boss fights when paired with the right Phantom Edge.

**Are Phantom Edges required, or can I just use a primary blade?**

You can clear most content using only a primary blade, but Phantom Edges add a critical layer of burst damage and status application — especially during Breached State windows on bosses. Players who skip Phantom Edges typically clear boss fights twenty to thirty percent slower than those who weave them into their combos.

**Which weapon has the highest damage in Phantom Blade Zero?**

The **Juggernaut** two-handed blade deals the highest single-hit damage, while the **White Serpent & Crimson Viper** twin set deals the highest sustained DPS over a full combo string. Soft Snake Sword is the top choice for damage-per-second-per-stamina-cost, which matters in longer encounters.

**Can you switch weapons mid-combat in Phantom Blade Zero?**

No — weapon swaps are only allowed at **Meditation Points**, which makes choosing your main blade for each region a meaningful strategic decision rather than a reactive one. Plan your loadout before engaging any major boss, because switching mid-fight is not possible.
