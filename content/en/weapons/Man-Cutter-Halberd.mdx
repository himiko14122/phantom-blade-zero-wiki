## Man-Cutter Halberd in Phantom Blade Zero: Moveset, Damage & Best Builds

The Man-Cutter is not a weapon for the faint of heart. In the hands of Soul, the condemned assassin with only 66 days to live, this massive halberd transforms every encounter into a high-stakes gamble. One wrong swing leaves you exposed. One well-timed charged slam deletes an entire health bar. If you have been searching for a weapon that rewards patience and punishes greed in equal measure, you have found it.

This guide breaks down everything you need to dominate with the Man-Cutter: its full moveset, damage profile, optimal builds, Phantom Edge pairings, Sha-Chi management, and which bosses crumble beneath its weight.

---

## What Is the Man-Cutter?

The Man-Cutter is a **Juggernaut-class heavy weapon** in Phantom Blade Zero — a category reserved for oversized arms that trade speed for devastating single-strike damage. It takes the form of a polearm-halberd hybrid with an elongated blade designed for wide, sweeping arcs and a weighted counterbalance that enables its signature charged overhead slam.

Unlike the fluid dual-blade chains of the White Serpent and Crimson Viper, or the mid-range versatility of the default Sanguine longsword, the Man-Cutter demands commitment. Every swing is a decision. Every miss is an invitation for enemies to punish you during the lengthy recovery frames.

The weapon’s in-game description hints at its brutal philosophy: *“Forged to cleave through ranks of armored foes. The wielder must become as immovable as the blade is unstoppable.”*

## Moveset Breakdown

Understanding the Man-Cutter’s complete moveset is essential before stepping into any serious fight. The weapon operates on a rhythm of setup and payoff.

### Light Attack Chain: Sweeping Slashes

The standard light attack sequence consists of three horizontal sweeps that cover a wide arc in front of Soul. The first two strikes come out at moderate speed for a Juggernaut weapon. The third sweep has extended range and carries enough stagger force to interrupt most non-Boss enemies mid-attack.

- **First Sweep:** Quickest startup, moderate damage, low Sha-Chi cost
- **Second Sweep:** Wider arc, hits enemies at Soul’s flanks
- **Third Sweep:** Extended forward reach, high stagger, can clip enemies attempting to dodge sideways

Use the light chain to control crowds of weaker enemies or to chip away at a Boss while waiting for an opening to charge.

### Heavy Attack: Charged Overhead Slam

This is the Man-Cutter’s identity. Hold the heavy attack input to raise the halberd overhead, charging for up to three distinct power levels. Release at the correct moment — or risk holding too long and whiffing entirely.

- **Level 1 Charge:** Quick overhead chop. Respectable damage. Useful as a combo ender.
- **Level 2 Charge:** Full-force slam with a small area-of-effect shockwave. Deals massive health and posture damage.
- **Level 3 Charge (Fully Charged):** The Man-Cutter ignites with visible energy. The slam creates a large shockwave, deals catastrophic damage, and automatically triggers a **Breached** state on any enemy whose Sha-Chi gauge is below half.

The Level 3 slam is your primary boss-killer. Landing it consistently separates novice wielders from true masters.

### Running Attack: Thrusting Lunge

While sprinting, press light attack to execute a forward thrust with deceptive range. This move closes distance faster than enemies expect and can catch retreating foes. It does not replace the charged slam for damage, but it is your best engagement tool against mobile bosses like TieSha the Frenzy.

### Aerial Attack: Descending Cleave

Jump and press heavy attack to bring the halberd down in a plunging strike. The damage is lower than a charged ground slam, but the vertical hitbox can strike flying enemies or interrupt leaping Boss attacks. Niche, but worth knowing.

### Ghostep Counter: Reaping Guillotine

After a successful Ghostep dodge — triggered by perfectly evading a Killer Move — press heavy attack to execute a unique counter. Soul spins the Man-Cutter in a full 360-degree arc before slamming it down. This move has hyper armor during the spin and guarantees a knockdown against human-sized enemies.

Mastering the Ghostep counter with the Man-Cutter turns defensive moments into your highest damage windows.

## Damage Profile and Speed Tradeoffs

The Man-Cutter occupies the extreme end of Phantom Blade Zero’s damage spectrum. No other weapon matches its single-hit potential, but no other weapon leaves you as vulnerable during recovery.

| Attribute | Man-Cutter Rating | Notes |
|-----------|-------------------|-------|
| Raw Damage | S | Highest per-hit damage in the game |
| Attack Speed | D | Slowest swing speed of all weapon classes |
| Range | A | Sweeping arcs cover wide areas; thrust extends reach |
| Stagger Power | S | Charged slams break posture in 1-2 hits |
| Sha-Chi Cost | High | Heavy attacks consume significant Sha-Chi |
| Recovery Frames | Long | Missed slams leave you exposed for 1+ seconds |

The speed tradeoff means you cannot play reactively with the Man-Cutter the way you might with the Soft Snake Sword or dual blades. You must anticipate enemy patterns, create openings through positioning, and commit to swings only when you are certain they will connect — or when you are willing to trade health for damage.

Against fast, aggressive bosses like the Chief Disciple of the Seven Stars, the Man-Cutter forces a deliberate, almost turn-based rhythm: dodge the flurry, punish with one charged slam, reset. Greed ends runs.

## Sha-Chi Efficiency and Management

Sha-Chi is the combat resource that governs heavy attacks, Phantom Edge usage, and defensive maneuvers in Phantom Blade Zero. The Man-Cutter is one of the most Sha-Chi-hungry weapons in the game.

A Level 3 charged slam consumes approximately 40% of a full Sha-Chi bar. Light attack chains consume less per swing but add up quickly over extended combos. If your Sha-Chi is fully depleted, you enter the **Breached** state — a vulnerability window where enemies deal increased damage and you cannot use heavy attacks or Phantom Edges.

### Tips for Sha-Chi Management with the Man-Cutter

- **Do not spam light chains.** Use one or two sweeps to test enemy reactions, then disengage.
- **Save Sha-Chi for charged slams.** The Level 3 slam’s damage-per-Sha-Chi ratio far exceeds anything else in the kit.
- **Use successful parries to regenerate Sha-Chi.** Parrying Brutal Moves (golden/orange indicators) restores a portion of your Sha-Chi bar. The Man-Cutter’s parry timing is tighter than lighter weapons, but the reward is worth learning.
- **Equip Sha-Chi-focused accessories.** Certain trinkets increase Sha-Chi regeneration speed or reduce heavy attack costs. Prioritize these in your build.

If you find yourself constantly Breached, you are either over-committing to combos or neglecting parry opportunities. Slow down.

## Best Phantom Edge Pairings

Phantom Edges are secondary weapons that Soul can deploy for quick attacks, combo extensions, or utility effects. With the Man-Cutter’s slow swing speed, your Phantom Edge choice should compensate for its weaknesses rather than doubling down on damage.

### S-Tier Pairings

**1. Stun Dagger (Phantom Edge — Blunt Type)**
A short-range thrust that deals minimal damage but applies a brief stun on hit. The stun window is just long enough to begin charging a Level 2 slam. Against aggressive bosses, this pairing creates openings that would otherwise not exist. Use it immediately after dodging a Killer Move to guarantee the stun connects.

**2. Gravity Tether (Phantom Edge — Utility Type)**
Deploys a projectile that pulls lightweight enemies toward Soul. Grouping enemies together sets up the Man-Cutter’s sweeping light attacks for maximum efficiency. Against single targets, the pull interrupts movement and buys you half a second — enough to start a heavy attack charge.

### A-Tier Pairings

**3. Flame Burst (Phantom Edge — Elemental Type)**
A short-range fire explosion that applies the Burn status over time. The damage-over-time effect compensates for the Man-Cutter’s low attack frequency. Apply Burn, then focus on landing one charged slam while the fire chips away.

**4. Phase Shift (Phantom Edge — Mobility Type)**
A quick backward dash that creates distance. The Man-Cutter’s worst matchups are enemies that stay in your face and punish slow startups. Phase Shift lets you reset to your ideal mid-range spacing instantly.

## Which Bosses Does the Man-Cutter Excel Against?

The Man-Cutter thrives against bosses with long recovery windows, predictable attack patterns, or mechanics that reward burst damage over sustained DPS.

### Ideal Matchups

**Coppermaul (Wan Jun)**
The massive hammer-wielding boss telegraphs his attacks heavily and leaves extended openings after every combo. A Level 3 charged slam during his recovery phase deletes chunks of his health bar. His slow movement speed also makes spacing trivial — you control the engagement distance entirely.

**Commander Cleave**
This mid-game boss summons adds periodically. The Man-Cutter’s sweeping light attacks clear the adds in two swings, and the charged slam punishes Commander Cleave’s lengthy overhead slash recovery. Equip Gravity Tether to group adds for even faster clearing.

**Red Wraith (Optional Boss)**
The Red Wraith encounter is a DPS race against its self-healing mechanic. The Man-Cutter’s burst damage outpaces the healing if you land charged slams consistently. The Stun Dagger pairing is especially valuable here — stun into Level 3 slam is your win condition.

### Challenging Matchups

**Chief Disciple of the Seven Stars**
This boss’s relentless multi-hit combos and short recovery windows make finding charge opportunities difficult. You will rely heavily on Ghostep counters and single light attacks. Consider switching to a faster weapon for this fight unless you are committed to the challenge.

**TieSha the Frenzy**
High mobility and unpredictable movement patterns make landing charged slams a coin flip. The Man-Cutter can work here, but only with Phase Shift equipped to manage distance.

## Tips for Landing Charged Attacks Consistently

Charged attacks are the Man-Cutter’s raison d’être, but whiffing them is punishing. These techniques will improve your accuracy dramatically.

### 1. Learn the Parry-to-Charge Rhythm

Against enemies with telegraphed Brutal Moves, parry the attack, then immediately begin charging. The parry stagger window is exactly long enough for a Level 2 charge against most enemies. Against larger bosses with longer recovery, you can reach Level 3.

### 2. Bait with Light Attacks

Throw one or two light sweeps to bait an enemy into attacking. Dodge backward, then begin charging while they are in recovery. This works consistently against humanoid enemies with aggressive AI.

### 3. Use Stun Dagger as a Setup Tool

The Stun Dagger Phantom Edge is not optional — it is your most reliable charge setup. Stun, charge, release. Repeat until victory.

### 4. Exploit Environmental Corners

Enemies backed against walls or obstacles cannot retreat from your slam’s shockwave. Position yourself to herd bosses into confined spaces before committing to a Level 3 charge.

### 5. Charge During Boss Phase Transitions

Many bosses have invulnerability periods during phase transitions. Use this time to begin charging. Release the moment they become vulnerable again for an instant massive hit.

## Best Builds for the Man-Cutter

### Build 1: The Immovable (Beginner-Friendly)

- **Phantom Edge:** Stun Dagger
- **Accessories:** Sha-Chi regeneration ring, damage reduction amulet
- **Strategy:** Parry often, stun when possible, charge to Level 2 consistently. Do not fish for Level 3 slams until you are comfortable with boss patterns. This build prioritizes survivability while you learn the weapon’s timing.

### Build 2: The Executioner (Advanced)

- **Phantom Edge:** Flame Burst or Gravity Tether
- **Accessories:** Charged attack damage boost, Sha-Chi cost reduction
- **Strategy:** Apply Burn or group enemies, then land Level 3 slams as often as possible. This build maximizes the Man-Cutter’s burst potential but leaves you fragile. Recommended for players who have mastered enemy movesets.

### Build 3: The Ghostep Specialist (Expert)

- **Phantom Edge:** Phase Shift
- **Accessories:** Ghostep damage boost, dodge window extension
- **Strategy:** Focus entirely on dodging Killer Moves and punishing with the Ghostep counter. Use Phase Shift to maintain ideal spacing. This build turns the Man-Cutter into a counter-attack weapon and excels against the fastest bosses.
