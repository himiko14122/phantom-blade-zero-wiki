## What Defines an S Tier Weapon in Phantom Blade Zero

A weapon only earns S tier status when it hits four benchmarks at once: top-end base damage, fast stance recovery, strong **Sha-Chi** channeling, and a meaningful contribution to the **Breached state** combo meter. Raw damage alone never qualifies a blade for the top of the tier list, because the action RPG rewards consistent punish windows over raw stat sticks. A blade that swings fast, recovers into the next stance quickly, and pushes enemies toward a Breached state will outperform a heavier sword with higher numbers that locks you into long animations.

Weapon weight class also feeds into the verdict. Light blades trade raw power for parry-friendly speed, mid-weight blades such as the **Sanguine** longsword hit a near-perfect balance, and heavy weapons like the **Jagged Edge** greatsword dominate single-target burst damage against bosses. Because every weapon in Phantom Blade Zero scales with Sha-Chi invested in its respective stance tree, the best S tier options always come with at least one high-tier stance synergy that flips their damage output during a **Ghostep** chain. The trick is picking a blade that complements your Sha-Chi build rather than fighting against it.

| Ranking Benchmark | Why It Matters | Weight It Carries |
|---|---|---|
| Base damage per swing | Sets raw kill speed | High |
| Stance recovery frames | Determines punish openings | High |
| **Sha-Chi** scaling ceiling | Late-game boss scaling | Very High |
| **Breached state** buildup | Stagger and riposte access | Very High |
| Move list versatility | Coverage of aerial and low-stance attacks | Medium |

Once a blade hits the top tier across every column above, it earns a permanent spot on the Phantom Blade Zero S tier weapons ranking and stays there regardless of patch cycles, since the design philosophy prioritizes stance fluidity over damage creep.

## Weapon Archetype Breakdown and Playstyle Fits

S tier weapons are not all built alike, and the archetype you commit to changes how you approach every encounter. Picking between a heavy blade, a twin blade set, or a chakram is a build decision that ripples through every other gear slot, which is why the section below breaks the ranking down by archetype before you commit.

### Heavy Blades: Sanguine, Jagged Edge, Juggernaut

Heavy blades dominate the frontline of any Phantom Blade Zero S tier weapons conversation because they all but guarantee stagger on standard enemies and crack open the **Breached state** on most bosses. The **Sanguine** longsword sits at the top of this archetype because its mid-weight frame lets it swing into high stance without locking you out of low-stance follow-ups. The **Jagged Edge** greatsword trades that flexibility for the highest single-target burst damage in the game, while the **Juggernaut** heavy blade rounds out the trio by clearing arena-sized mob waves with a single arc.

| Heavy Blade | Damage Tier | Stance Speed | Best Counter Matchup |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sanguine** | Mid | Fast | Parry-hungry humanoid bosses |
| **Jagged Edge** | Very High | Slow | Slow-swinging beast bosses |
| **Juggernaut** | High | Medium | Mob phases between bosses |

Heavy blades all share one weakness: their recovery frames punish you hard against fast multi-hitting bosses. Pair the **Sanguine** with the parry tips in our [how to parry breakdown](/en/guides/how-to-parry/) so you can convert those long recoveries into counter opportunities, since that interaction alone moves the longsword from rank two to rank one in practice.

### Dual Blades and Twin Sets: White Serpent and Crimson Viper

Twin blades operate on a completely different philosophy, since the **White Serpent** and **Crimson Viper** set wins encounters by out-pacing the stagger meter rather than breaking it in a single swing. Both blades push the **Breached state** meter faster than any other option in the game, and the slash hit-count from a single twin-blade combo is what enables that. Mid stance is their native home, since the twin-blade finisher from mid stance chains straight into a Ghostep cancel, which turns a defensive dodge into an offensive punish opportunity in one motion.

| Twin Blade | Combo Hit Count | Breached Buildup | Stance Synergy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **White Serpent** | 6 per combo | Very High | Mid stance finisher |
| **Crimson Viper** | 7 per combo | Maximum | Mid stance finisher |
| Pair Bonus | +2 hit chain | +25% buildup | Doubles on switch |

The twin blade set looks weaker on a pure damage-per-swing chart, which is why raw-tier-list sites often rank it lower than the heavy blades. In actual fights against multi-phase bosses with hard damage windows, the **White Serpent** and **Crimson Viper** combination is the fastest way to trigger a Breached state, which is the only path to a guaranteed riposte on most late-game encounters.

### Ranged and Utility: Seamless Death and Soft Snake Sword

The **Seamless Death** chakram is the only ranged entry across all Phantom Blade Zero S tier weapons, and the **Soft Snake Sword** curved blade covers the aerial niche that every other top-tier entry cannot reach. The chakram shines during multi-enemy encounters when off-screen adds would normally break your combo flow, because its throw-and-return arc hits targets without forcing you out of stance. The **Soft Snake Sword** curved blade finishes aerial strings faster than any straight blade, and that single trait makes it irreplaceable for fights that spend large windows airborne.

| Utility Weapon | Range | Air Combo Tier | Hidden Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Seamless Death** | Long | Medium | Off-screen add control |
| **Soft Snake Sword** | Medium | Maximum | Aerial finisher reach |

## How to Unlock and Upgrade S Tier Weapons

Acquiring Phantom Blade Zero S tier weapons is a mix of story progression, side content, and boss soul trades, and knowing the exact path saves you from burning hours farming the wrong NPC. The base game lists over 30 primary blades on the [official store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4115450/Phantom_Blade_Zero/), and the S tier entries are typically locked behind chapter milestones, hidden vendors, or boss soul exchanges rather than random drops. Plan upgrades around your Sha-Chi points since every weapon scales off the stance tree you invest in, which is why upgrading the wrong blade is the most common waste of late-game mats.

| Weapon | Unlock Path | Upgrade Mats | Sha-Chi Tree Needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sanguine** | Chapter 2 main story | Standard forging + 1 boss soul | High stance |

Most players waste their first boss souls on whichever S tier blade they pulled first, but the optimal order is to commit your earliest mats to the **Sanguine** longsword since it covers the widest stance range with the smoothest learning curve. Save the rare boss souls until you reach Chapter 5 so the **Jagged Edge** and **Juggernaut** unlocks are both available, because spending a soul on the greatsword before the Juggernaut unlock means locking yourself out of the heavy blade for the rest of that run.

The upgrade ceiling on each S tier weapon is capped by how deeply you invest in its stance tree, so pair every upgrade with a focused Sha-Chi grind rather than spreading points across all three stances. ## Matching S Tier Weapons to Your Combat Style

Every Phantom Blade Zero S tier weapons entry maps to one or two combat identities, and matching the blade to your instincts is what separates a clean run from a struggling one. Pick a weapon that aligns with how you instinctively handle pressure instead of forcing yourself into a damage chart, because the action RPG rewards commitment to a single tempo over switching mid-fight.

### Aggressive Burst Playstyle

Players who chase openings and live in the enemy's face lean hard into the **Jagged Edge** greatsword or the **Juggernaut** heavy blade, since both weapons convert high stance aggression into the fastest single-target kill in the game. The **Sanguine** longsword is the safer aggressive pick because it lets you bail out of a committed swing into low stance if the parry window fails. None of these match the **Seamless Death** chakram because ranged weapons punish hesitation rather than reward pressure.

### Defensive Counter Playstyle

Players who bait enemies into attacking and punish through parries will do best on the **Sanguine** longsword, the **Soft Snake Sword**, or the **White Serpent** twin blade set. All three entries open consistent parry windows because their mid-stance speed leaves enough frames between swings for a counter attempt, which is the core loop covered in the [how to parry guide](/en/guides/how-to-parry/). The **Crimson Viper** twin blade pairs especially well with counter play because its seven-hit combo into a mid-stance finisher converts every blocked swing into a stagger fill.

| Playstyle Identity | First Pick | Second Pick | Skip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aggressive burst | **Jagged Edge** | **Juggernaut** | Chakrams |
| Defensive counter | **Sanguine** | **White Serpent** | Greatsword |
| Aerial focus | **Soft Snake Sword** | **Sanguine** | Heavy blades |
| Ranged control | **Seamless Death** | Twin blade set | Greatsword |

If your instinct is to stay grounded, the **Sanguine** carries more value than any other Phantom Blade Zero S tier weapons entry because it is the only top-tier blade that fits both aggressive and counter identities without any gear swap. If you prefer staying airborne, commit to the **Soft Snake Sword** early since it does not appear in any other category, and that exclusivity makes it the strongest pick for an aerial-focused build.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What makes a weapon S tier in Phantom Blade Zero?**

A weapon earns S tier only when it tops every ranking benchmark at once, including base damage, stance recovery, **Sha-Chi** scaling, and **Breached state** contribution. The **Sanguine** longsword is the clearest example because it scores in the top tier of every column.

**Which Phantom Blade Zero S tier weapon is best for beginners?**

The **Sanguine** longsword is the most forgiving top-tier pick because its mid-speed swings open parry windows naturally, and it covers all three stances without locking you out of recovery frames. Pair it with the basics from the [beginner guide](/en/guides/beginner-guide/) for a smoother first chapter.

**Are twin blades viable for endgame bosses in Phantom Blade Zero?**

Yes. The **White Serpent** and **Crimson Viper** twin blade set is the fastest way to fill the Breached state meter in the game, which is the only reliable path to a guaranteed riposte on multi-phase bosses. They need a mid-stance focused Sha-Chi build to fully unlock, but no other pairing matches their stagger push.

**Where do you get Sha-Chi upgrade materials for S tier weapons?**

Sha-Chi scrolls and forging tokens drop from mini-bosses, bounty boards, and chapter completion rewards scattered across the open zones. Save the rare boss souls for **Juggernaut** and **Jagged Edge** since those weapons cannot be forged without them.

**Should you use chakrams in Phantom Blade Zero?**

The **Seamless Death** chakram is the only ranged entry across all Phantom Blade Zero S tier weapons, and it excels at punishing enemies that block or dodge while you stay in stance. It is niche, but for fights where off-screen adds keep breaking your combo, no other S tier option handles that pressure as cleanly.
