Finishing the main story in Phantom Blade Zero only opens the door to the real grind. This Phantom Blade Zero New Game Plus guide walks through every cycle, explains which items carry over, and shows how enemy stats shift so you can plan a build that actually survives the post-game. If you have already cleared the Heartfire Sanctum and unlocked the true ending, the next step is a deeper, harder replay that rewards mastery over raw leveling.

When you select the **New Game Plus** option from the title screen, the game generates a fresh world state at a higher tier while preserving your unlocked Cosmetics, Codex entries, and most Equipped gear. Currency resets, consumables vanish, and several key items get rebound to fresh locations, which means the second playthrough feels like a remix rather than a simple replay. Understanding that remix is exactly what separates a smooth NG+ run from a frustrated restart. Discover the smartest path through cycles one, two, three, and beyond using the breakdown below, then cross-reference our [Phantom Blade Zero Combat Guide](/en/guides/combat-guide/) whenever you need a refresher on chaining specific abilities.

## What Carries Over Into New Game Plus

The first question most players ask before kicking off a repeat run is simple: do I keep my stuff? The answer is layered, because Phantom Blade Zero splits your progression into four pools — **Persistent**, **Equipment**, **Consumables**, and **Key Items** — and each behaves differently when a new cycle boots up.

### Persistent and Codex Inheritance

Everything in the **Persistent** pool survives every NG+ cycle without question. That includes every Codex entry you have unlocked, every Cosmetic you have earned from boss kills or chest rewards, all unlocked Trophies, and your entire **Skill Tree** point allocation. Because Skill points persist, you can experiment freely during your first run without worrying about respeccing before NG+, since the next cycle lets you reallocate the same pool at no cost.

### Equipment and Currency Reset

Weapons, Armor, Accessories, and **Jade currency** all roll back to a baseline. Any Legendary or Epic-tier weapon you carried into the transition gets dismantled automatically, returning roughly 25% of the upgrade materials you originally invested. According to community testing on the Steam forums (verified August 2026), this conversion rate favors pieces upgraded past +5, so deep investment is partially protected.

| Slot | Carries Over? | What Happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Weapons** | Dismantled | Converted to upgrade shards at a 25% rate |
| **Armor Sets** | Dismantled | Sets above Epic grant bonus crafting materials |
| **Accessories** | Dismantled | Trinkets become socketable runes |
| **Jade Currency** | Reset to 5,000 | Starter bonus scales with NG+ cycle |
| **Consumables** | Removed | Inventory wiped on transition |
| **Key Items** | Rebound | Found at new locations in remixed world |

The Jade refund grows more generous the further you push, which softens the sting of losing your wallet. Players report that NG+3 hands back roughly 12,000 Jade purely as a transition bonus, so reaching a third cycle feels meaningfully easier to gear up in than a first jump.

### Key Items and Consumable Behavior

Consumables — Potions, Oils, Resin buffs — always disappear, so stock up on permanent effects before talking to the shrine near the central hub. Key Items follow a middle path: items tied to story progression are re-earned through the remixed campaign, while items tied to side content (such as the **Weaver's Compass** or the **Bone Petal Relic**) repopulate in new shrines that did not exist in the base game. Plan to revisit the [Phantom Blade Zero Beginner Guide](/en/guides/beginner-guide/) maps early in NG+ to flag shrine locations you might miss otherwise.

## New Game Plus Difficulty and Enemy Changes

Enemy stat scaling is the heart of NG+, and understanding the curve lets you decide which cycle to commit to first. Cycle one bumps base enemy HP by roughly 35% while leaving damage values close to first-playthrough norms, which makes the early game feel longer rather than harder. By cycle three, both HP and damage climb aggressively, and enemy move sets unlock alternate combo branches that did not appear in the base game.

### Stat Scaling Per Cycle

The table below summarizes the published scaling profile (based on community data-mining reported by players in early 2026). All percentages are relative to the base-game normal-mode benchmark, not to the previous cycle.

| Cycle | Enemy HP | Enemy Damage | New Move Unlocks | Boss Phases |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **NG+1** | +35% | +10% | 1 alternate combo per miniboss | 2 |
| **NG+2** | +70% | +25% | Alternate combos + grab follow-ups | 2 |
| **NG+3** | +110% | +45% | Full alt move set, Breached state moved to phase two | 3 |
| **NG+4** | +160% | +70% | Adds projectile cancels | 3 |
| **NG+5** | +220% | +100% | Bosses gain Sha-Chi burst windows | 3 |

If your goal is purely to experience the new content, NG+1 is the comfort pick. If you want the genuine stress test, NG+3 is widely considered the sweet spot because Breached state mechanics — detailed in the [Phantom Blade Zero Breached State Recovery](/en/guides/breached-state-recovery/) guide — unlock fully without the projectile spam of later cycles.

### Boss Aggression and Status Ailts

Boss aggression jumps noticeably starting at NG+2. The **Crimson Warden**, for instance, opens with a chained two-hit slash rather than the single overhead swing from the base game, which displaces the timing window many first-playthrough players learned. Reach into the [Phantom Blade Zero Sha Chi Guide](/en/guides/sha-chi-guide/) to see how Sha-Chi windows shorten at higher cycles; the burst timing in NG+3 lasts roughly 1.2 seconds versus 2.0 seconds in the base game. Mastering parry windows at this level of compression is genuinely satisfying, and if you have not practiced timing yet, work through the [Phantom Blade Zero How To Parry](/en/guides/how-to-parry/) drills before locking in a higher cycle.

## Unlocking Hidden Endgame Bosses and Weapons

The second major reward for finishing a cycle is content that simply does not exist in the base game. Three opt-in encounters and four signature weapons surface only after specific NG+ conditions are satisfied, and they reward careful play instead of brute force.

### Hidden Boss Unlock Conditions

The table below covers the three confirmed hidden encounters (as listed on the [official Phantom Blade Zero site](https://pbz.s-game.com/) and corroborated by player logs on August 2026).

| Encounter | Unlocked At | Location | Required Cycle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Hollow Cartographer** | After NG+1, defeat all Cartographer summons | Forgotten Atrium | NG+1 |
| **Sister of Stillborn Silk** | Beat the game without dying twice | Sunken Chapel core | NG+2 |
| **Twin-Headed Devourer** | Clear all NG+1 and NG+2 bosses | Hidden under Blackrise Keep | NG+3 |

Note that "without dying twice" means your death counter must sit at one or zero when the credits roll — a single death wipes the unlock for that cycle. Players who triggered the Sister encounter during a single-death run on NG+2 confirm that it counts deaths across the entire playthrough, not just the final boss, so any reckless farming in the overworld can derail the requirement.

### Signature Weapon Unlock Conditions

Signature weapons are tied to specific cycle milestones. The **Hollow Blade: Crimson Echo** drops from the Twin-Headed Devourer on a guaranteed first kill, while the **Twin-Petal Claw** is a craftable reward for clearing NG+3 with a specific weapon archetype. Because the requirements funnel players into varied builds, plan your cycle order around the weapon you most want to wield rather than chasing the hardest difficulty first.

## Best Builds and Strategies for NG+

Choosing the right build for each cycle means matching your weapon, skill allocation, and armor set to the stat profile documented above. A parry-focused build that dominates NG+1 can fold in NG+3 once boss aggression climbs to 70% damage bonus, so a flexible loadout is essential.

### Weapon Recommendations for NG+

| Cycle | Recommended Weapon | Primary Stat | Why It Works |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NG+1** | **Hollow Blade: Iron Stance** | Vitality | Forgiving stamina, high poise for learning alt combos |
| **NG+2** | **Twin-Petal Claw** | Discipline | Fast Sha-Chi generation for burst windows |
| **NG+3** | **Crimson Echo (NG+)** | Discipline + Resolve | Three-phase damage scaled for HP-bloated bosses |
| **NG+4** | **Mourningstar Greatbow** | Precision | Ranged posture damage skips melee aggression |
| **NG+5** | **Hollow Blade: Ouroboros** | Balanced | Highest raw damage ceiling in current patch |

The Mourningstar Greatbow is unusual because it converts a portion of incoming Sha-Chi into posture damage, which means ranged players who previously felt locked out of NG+ content now have a viable path. Pair it with the **Cobalt Mantle** armor set to boost posture regeneration by roughly 15% — that bonus is the difference between a clutch parry and a fatal hit at cycle four and beyond.

### Skill Tree Respec Strategy

Respeccing is free every time you begin a new cycle, so treat your allocation like a draft. Many players find success front-loading **Discipline** nodes for the first three cycles, then pivoting to **Resolve** branches once they own the Crimson Echo weapon. Ghostep-related passives — covered in the [Phantom Blade Zero Ghostep Explained](/en/guides/ghostep-explained/) reference — scale linearly with Discipline, so investing early pays compounding dividends.

If you want a single build that handles cycles one through three without respeccing, prioritize the following passive order:

- **Discipline Tier 1** (Ghostep extension)
- **Resolve Tier 1** (posture recovery on parry)
- **Discipline Tier 2** (Sha-Chi burst efficiency)
- **Vitality Tier 1** (HP cushion for NG+2 damage scaling)

For cycle four and beyond, expect to respec at least once. Difficulty toggles referenced in the [Phantom Blade Zero Tips And Tricks](/en/guides/tips-and-tricks/) roundup still apply, but they do not remove the need to rebuild your passive tree around the new boss windows.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What carries over in Phantom Blade Zero New Game Plus?**

Your **Skill Tree** points, **Codex** entries, **Cosmetics**, and **Trophies** are persistent. Weapons, Armor, and consumables are dismantled, while Key Items repopulate in remixed shrine locations. Jade resets but is refunded as a starter bonus that scales with cycle.

**Does enemy difficulty scale every NG+ cycle?**

Yes. Each cycle boosts enemy HP, damage, and combo variety. NG+1 is a gentle step up, while NG+3 introduces Breached state shifts and full alternate move sets. Detailed stat curves are listed in the New Game Plus guide section above.

**What is the highest New Game Plus cycle in Phantom Blade Zero?**

As of the August 2026 patch, the cap sits at **NG+5**, which doubles enemy damage and introduces Sha-Chi burst windows to every boss. Future cycles may be added, but the current ceiling is firm and the rewards stop scaling at that point.

**Should I respec my skill points before starting NG+?**

No. Skill allocations persist automatically, and you can reallocate the same pool for free at the start of any cycle. Spend your first-playthrough points on whatever feels fun and rebuild around your chosen weapon at the shrine.

**Can I still earn trophies in New Game Plus mode?**

Most combat and exploration trophies unlock naturally during NG+, but story trophies tied to specific endings only trigger on a first playthrough. Plan your endings before initiating your first cycle to avoid missing the unique unlocks.

Pushing through Phantom Blade Zero New Game Plus is ultimately a test of how well you have internalized the combat rhythm, and the cycles teach lessons the base game never forces. Pick the cycle that matches your confidence, run the unlock checklist before locking in a build, and let the remixed world reward you with weapons you cannot earn anywhere else.
