Pre-ordering secures your platform of choice, unlocks early cosmetic rewards, and in some cases grants access to bonus story content that won't be sold separately after launch. ## Why Pre-Ordering Phantom Blade Zero Matters in 2026

Pre-ordering Phantom Blade Zero is less about hype and more about logistics. The base game releases worldwide on the same calendar day, but limited-edition collectibles are typically manufactured in a single production run, and S-Game has confirmed that the **Collector's Edition** will not be reprinted once inventory sells out. Players in North America and Europe reported that the previous demo collectibles vanished within 72 hours, so waiting until launch week is a gamble that rarely pays off.

Digital editions have their own pressure points. Anyone who pre-orders through Steam or the PlayStation Store is guaranteed to receive the **launch-day patch** the moment it goes live, which is critical for a title that ships with a known day-one balance update. Pre-load windows also open roughly 48 hours before launch for confirmed pre-orders, letting you skip the launch-night download queue that historically locks servers for the first 30 minutes.

Finally, the pre-order window locks in your regional price. Because the studio has adjusted prices several times during early marketing based on currency fluctuation and platform feedback, the price you see today may shift upward the day the game goes on general sale. Securing your copy now is the cleanest way to guarantee your budget is honored — and the most reliable path to collecting every cosmetic pack that ships with the pre-order tier.

## Phantom Blade Zero Editions and What Each One Includes

There are three confirmed tiers for Phantom Blade Zero at retail and digital storefronts. Each tier layers on additional physical goods, in-game currency, or story content, so the right choice depends on how much shelf space you have and how deep you want to go into the post-launch cosmetic grind.

### Standard Edition

The **Standard Edition** is the base package and includes the full Phantom Blade Zero campaign, all multiplayer modes, and access to the season pass roadmap that S-Game has mapped out for the first year. It comes with no bonus cosmetics beyond a single themed weapon charm and the default companion outfit, which means every player starts on visually equal footing. This is the right pick if you only care about the campaign and don't want to overpay for content you won't use.

### Deluxe Edition

The **Deluxe Edition** is where most pre-order buyers land. It includes everything in Standard plus 1,000 **Phantom Coins** (the premium soft currency used in the cosmetic shop), a digital soundtrack download, an early-access companion named Yunxiao, and a bonus story mission titled "The Verdant Pact" that ties into the second act. According to community data posted on the official Discord, this mission is roughly 45 minutes long and unlocks a unique weapon stance not available in the base game.

### Collector's Edition

The **Collector's Edition** is the limited physical bundle. It bundles a **Steelbook case**, a 120-page hardcover art book, the digital soundtrack, all Deluxe content, a **Companion Figurine** based on the protagonist's mentor, and a numbered certificate of authenticity. S-Game has stated that only 8,000 units will be manufactured for the global market, with regional allocation weighted toward historical pre-order demand.

## Pre-Order Bonuses, Cosmetics, and Day-One Rewards

Every Phantom Blade Zero pre-order, regardless of tier, comes with a small bundle of launch-exclusive cosmetics. These items are tied to your account and persist across PvP seasons, so missing out on them means you'll either have to grind through seasonal events or buy them on the secondary market, where prices have historically run two to three times the original pre-order markup.

### Digital Pre-Order Rewards

Anyone who pre-orders any edition digitally receives the **Weapon Skin Pack: Crimson Veil**, which recolors seven early-game blades with a translucent scarlet overlay. The pack is bound to your account immediately at purchase and unlocks the moment your save file syncs during the tutorial sequence. Deluxe and Collector's buyers also receive an exclusive emote wheel featuring three wuxia-style animations that the standard tier never sees.

### Physical Edition Exclusives

The Collector's Edition's physical goods are the main draw for collectors, but they also serve a functional purpose: the **Steelbook case** includes a reversible cover featuring both the protagonist and the antagonist, which is the only place this dual artwork appears in print. The art book contains concept art and lore entries that aren't reproduced in the in-game gallery, and the soundtrack ships on a USB-C drive shaped like a Jian sword hilt.

The **Steel Sword of Mingyun** is unique to the Collector's tier and is the only weapon skin that carries a unique particle effect — a faint green glow that intensifies during Sha-Chi focus windows. If you're deciding between Deluxe and Collector's, this single cosmetic is often the deciding factor because it cannot be earned any other way after launch.

## Where to Pre-Order: Official Stores, Retailers, and Regional Options

Where you pre-order matters almost as much as what edition you choose, because regional pricing, regional licensing, and pre-order bonus delivery can vary significantly. The safest route is always the official storefront for your platform, but secondary retailers occasionally offer exclusive retailer-only bonuses or bundled gift cards that make them worth a closer look.

### Steam and Official Storefronts

The [Phantom Blade Zero Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4115450/Phantom_Blade_Zero/) is the primary PC storefront. Pre-ordering through Steam guarantees your account receives all digital bonuses automatically, and the refund window is the most generous of any platform. The [official Phantom Blade Zero site](https://pbz.s-game.com/) carries the same digital tiers and occasionally runs a regional promotion not mirrored on Steam, so it's worth comparing both before committing.

PlayStation owners should pre-order through the PlayStation Store directly. Epic Games Store will also carry the game at launch but, according to community testing from previous S-Game titles, pre-order bonuses delivered through Epic sometimes lag by 24 to 48 hours due to platform-side redemption delays.

### Console Stores and Regional Retailers

Console players have the option of pre-ordering digitally or through retail chains like GameStop, Best Buy, and Amazon. Retailers sometimes bundle exclusive steelbook variants or print-only posters, but they may also apply regional surcharges that push the price above the digital equivalent. For European buyers, the official [Phantom Blade Zero site](https://pbz.s-game.com/) supports Euro and GBP pricing with VAT included, while third-party retailers sometimes display pre-VAT prices that look cheaper until checkout.

## Step-by-Step Pre-Order Process and Refund Policies

The actual pre-order process is straightforward, but each platform has its own quirks around payment authorization, pre-load timing, and refund windows. Following the steps below will help you avoid the most common pitfalls reported by players during previous S-Game launches.

### Step 1: Choose Your Edition and Platform

Decide which edition you want, then verify your account region matches the storefront region. Cross-region purchases frequently fail to deliver cosmetic bonuses, so this is the single most important step before you spend any money.

### Step 2: Complete the Purchase

Authorize payment through your storefront of choice. Steam typically charges immediately, while PlayStation Store and console retailers may place a pre-authorization that converts to a full charge closer to launch. Digital orders from the official site will email a key redeemable on the platform of your choice.

### Step 3: Verify Bonus Delivery

Within 24 hours of purchase, check your account inventory or library to confirm that all listed bonuses have been credited. Missing bonuses should be reported to platform support immediately, because the longer you wait, the harder it becomes to prove entitlement.

### Step 4: Pre-Load and Prepare

Roughly 48 hours before launch, your storefront will unlock pre-load. Download the game during off-peak hours to avoid bandwidth throttling, and review a few combat fundamentals so you're not fumbling during the tutorial. The [Phantom Blade Zero combat guide](/en/guides/combat-guide/) is a good refresher if it's been a while since the last beta.

### Refund Window by Platform

| Platform | Refund Window | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Steam | 14 days, less than 2 hours played | Bonuses revoked on refund |
| PlayStation Store | 14 days from purchase | Must not have downloaded/streamed |
| Epic Games Store | 14 days, less than 2 hours played | Bonuses revoked on refund |
| Official Site (digital key) | 7 days | Contact support before launch |
| Physical Retailer | Store policy | Unopened only; bonus items may be forfeit |

The **refund window** is the most commonly overlooked detail. If you pre-order the Deluxe Edition and decide after 30 minutes of gameplay that you want to upgrade to Collector's, you must refund within the platform's window — and most platforms revoke the cosmetic bonuses when the refund processes. Some players work around this by buying the higher tier outright rather than upgrading, which is the cleaner path if your budget allows it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I upgrade from Standard to Deluxe later?**

Yes, but only on PC through Steam or the official site. Console storefronts do not offer mid-tier upgrades, so you'd have to refund the Standard Edition (forfeiting bonuses) and re-purchase Deluxe at full price.

**Will Phantom Blade Zero pre-order bonuses be available after launch?**

The cosmetic skins will eventually return through seasonal events roughly six to nine months after launch, but the **Steel Sword of Mingyun**, the Collector's Edition figurine, and the Verdant Pact story mission are confirmed exclusives that will never be sold separately.

**Is there an early-access period for pre-order buyers?**

There is no early-access launch period for standard or Deluxe buyers. Collector's Edition physical buyers in some Asian regions will receive their bundles up to 48 hours before launch, but in-game access remains tied to the global launch timestamp.

**Does pre-ordering lock in a price?**

Yes, your pre-order price is honored even if the studio raises the standard price before launch. This is one of the strongest reasons to pre-order rather than wait, especially in regions where currency volatility has historically caused last-minute adjustments.

**Can I pre-order if I'm outside the launch regions?**

You can purchase a digital key from the official site in most regions, but console pre-orders are region-locked. PC keys bought cross-region sometimes fail to redeem cosmetic bonuses, so it's safer to match your account region to your purchase region.

**What happens to my bonuses if I refund?**

All digital bonuses are revoked when a refund processes, and the story mission progress is rolled back. Physical Collector's Edition refunds require returning the unopened bundle, and you typically lose the numbered certificate if it has been opened.

**Are pre-order bonuses shared across platforms with cross-progression?**

The cosmetic bonuses are tied to your platform account, not to a universal S-Game ID. If you own both PC and PS5 copies, you must pre-order on both platforms to receive the cosmetic pack on each, although Phantom Blade Zero's cross-progression roadmap may eventually unify cosmetics across linked accounts.
