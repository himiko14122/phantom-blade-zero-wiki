## What the Wishlist Milestone Actually Tracks

The Steam wishlist counter is one of the most-watched pre-launch metrics in PC gaming, because every wishlist click feeds directly into Valve's recommendation algorithm and influences how often a title appears in the **Discover Queue**, **Recommended For You** rail, and front-page curation slots. When a game like Phantom Blade Zero crosses a noticeable round-number threshold, it is not just a vanity stat — it changes the visibility curve heading into launch week.

Valve does not publish a single canonical "milestone number" for every game, but community trackers and the developer-side Steamworks dashboard surface a few widely referenced bands. The bands below are based on publicly visible signals reported by players who monitor the [Phantom Blade Zero on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4115450/Phantom_Blade_Zero/) store page over time.
