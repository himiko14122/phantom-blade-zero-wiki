## Who Is TieSha the Frenzy?

TieSha the Frenzy is a **mid-to-late game boss** in Phantom Blade Zero, encountered deep within the Huangxing district storyline where the player's quest intersects with the Asura warlord's raiders. She functions as a skill check for players who have already mastered basic parry timing but haven't yet adapted to sustained aggression, because her entire moveset revolves around chaining rapid, overlapping combos that punish hesitation. Unlike the heavier hitters in the roster such as Wan Jun Coppermaul or Huangxing the Sunken, TieSha wields twin short blades and relies on **speed rather than raw damage**, which makes her one of the most technically demanding duels in the game.

According to community testing from the August 2026 preview build, her encounter is technically optional but tied to a unique weapon reward that scales cleanly into the late-game Sha-Chi economy. Because she appears right before several punishing story checkpoints, learning her fight order also preps your reflexes for the harder duels waiting on the other side. If you want a refresher on the broader combat fundamentals before committing, the [Phantom Blade Zero Beginner Guide](/en/guides/beginner-guide/) covers the parry, dodge, and Sha-Chi foundations that this encounter assumes you already know.

## TieSha the Frenzy Attack Pattern Breakdown

TieSha's combat loop is built around three distinct behavioral phases, each escalating her aggression. Learning how to read her telegraphs is the single most important factor in this Phantom Blade Zero TieSha the Frenzy boss fight, because her recovery windows shrink dramatically between phases, and the visual cues shift from subtle to almost imperceptible by the end.

### Phase 1: Frenzy Swipes

During the opening third of the encounter, TieSha uses **horizontal slash chains** that travel in two-to-four hit sequences. Each slash leaves a faint red afterimage that lingers for roughly 0.4 seconds, giving skilled players a clear visual parry cue. The combo typically ends with a leaping **Aerial Slam** that crashes both blades into the ground, opening a brief but reliable punish window. If you are still refining your deflection timing, the dedicated [how to parry walkthrough](/en/guides/how-to-parry/) breaks down the frame data that applies directly to this opening phase.

### Phase 2: Mad Cascade

Once her health drops below roughly 60 percent, TieSha enters what the community calls the **Mad Cascade**, where she begins mixing in unblockable thrust attacks and dash-canceled follow-ups. Her **Berserk Meter** fills visibly on her weapon, and once it maxes out she gains hyperarmor on every attack for about ten seconds, meaning light hits will no longer stagger her mid-combo. This is the phase where most first attempts end in failure, because players instinctively try to trade blows instead of disengaging.

### Phase 3: Wraith Form

At around 30 percent HP, TieSha detaches her blades and enters **Wraith Form**, where she teleports in short bursts and performs ranged slash projectiles that track your position for a short distance. During this phase, your Breached state recovery timing becomes essential, because she can chain you into the ground floor if you heal at the wrong moment. According to player reports, her teleport distance scales with how aggressively you pursued her in the previous phase, which means a more passive phase 2 actually leads to a more manageable phase 3.

| Phase | Health Threshold | Key Attack | Punish Window | Recommended Response |
|-------|------------------|------------|---------------|----------------------|
| Frenzy Swipes | 100%–60% | Aerial Slam | ~1.2 seconds | Parry chain, then 2 light hits |
| Mad Cascade | 60%–30% | Hyperarmor Slash | ~0.6 seconds | Dodge only, deflect red thrusts |
| Wraith Form | 30%–0% | Tracking Projectiles | ~0.9 seconds after dodge | Strafe perpendicular, then punish |
| Berserk Burst | Triggered at meter max | Ground Spinner | None — must deflect | Parry on the second rotation |

## Required Loadout and Sha-Chi Setup

Because TieSha's encounter demands tight windows and stamina-heavy play, your build must support sustained recovery and reliable stagger damage. The recommended loadout focuses on weapons that can interrupt her hyperarmor phase without burning your entire healing window in the process. Players who already cleared the Chief Disciple of Seven Stars formation will recognize that TieSha tests the same disciplines at a faster tempo.

### Recommended Weapon Types

A **medium-weight curved blade** such as the Ember Crescent works exceptionally well in this matchup, because it deals solid poise damage while keeping your dodge i-frames generous. Heavier warhammers can technically stagger her out of hyperarmor, but they leave you vulnerable to her dash-canceled follow-ups during the Mad Cascade. For players who prefer the deflection-first approach covered in the broader [combat guide](/en/guides/combat-guide/), a katana-class weapon with the Swift Deflection art lets you chain parries into a counter-riposte combo that lines up cleanly with her Aerial Slam recovery.

### Sha-Chi Allocation

Investing roughly 40 percent of your **Sha-Chi points** into Vitality, 35 percent into Stamina, and 25 percent into raw damage output is the most reliable spread, according to community data from the Steam preview cohort. Avoid pure glass-cannon builds for this particular fight, because the Wraith Form phase has enough chip damage to delete a squishy character in two unblocked hits. If you need a refresher on how Sha-Chi scales per upgrade tier, the [Phantom Blade Zero Sha Chi Guide](/en/guides/sha-chi-guide/) walks through the math in detail.

## Step-by-Step Strategy to Defeat TieSha the Frenzy

Once you understand her patterns and have the right gear, the actual fight becomes a rhythm exercise. The key is staying calm through her aggression spikes and capitalizing on the recovery gaps that open between combos, rather than trying to out-DPS her outright, because her health pool is tuned for a longer duel.

### Opening Phase Tactics

Start the fight by **buffing or applying your Sha-Chi art** while she's performing her idle animation, then bait the first Frenzy Swipe combo by sidestepping backward to create distance. You want to deflect only the second and third hits of her chain, because deflecting the opener leaves you open to the Aerial Slam follow-up that almost always follows. After the slam lands, dash forward and land two light attacks, then back away before the next combo starts. Resist the urge to chase her across the arena, because the larger gap gives her space to trigger the dash-cancel mixups earlier.

### Surviving the Mad Cascade

The Mad Cascade is where most players die in this Phantom Blade Zero TieSha the Frenzy boss encounter, because her hyperarmor slash ignores your standard stagger attempts and trades will always favor her. The trick is to **not commit to trades** during this phase and instead play fully reactive — deflect only the thrust attacks (which are red-telegraphed) and dodge everything else, because her slash chains can be sidestepped cleanly if you time the roll with the second hit. Once her Berserk Meter empties, she will stagger for roughly one second, and that single second is your only safe punish window before the hyperarmor timer resets.

### Closing the Wraith Form Phase

When she transitions into Wraith Form, **immediately create distance** and use ranged Sha-Chi arts to whittle her down while she teleports around the arena. Her tracking projectiles can be sidestepped by strafing perpendicular to her line of sight, and each dodge gives you about 0.9 seconds to land one heavy attack before repositioning. Save your healing items for this phase, because her teleport bursts make mid-combo healing extremely risky and will usually lead into a Breached state you didn't plan for. Finishing her under 30 percent HP is largely a stamina-management exercise, so don't burn your meter on flashy arts you don't need.

## Common Mistakes and Recovery Tips

Even experienced players slip up against TieSha, mostly because her pacing is so different from the slower bosses earlier in the campaign such as Red Wraith or Commander Cleave. Avoiding the following errors will dramatically improve your clear rate on your first few attempts, and each one is based on patterns seen across hundreds of community upload runs.

| Mistake | Why It Fails | Recommended Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----------------|
| Healing during her dash-cancel | She punishes heal animations | Heal only after a confirmed knockdown |
| Greedy parry chains | Drains stamina mid-fight | Parry twice, then disengage |
| Ignoring Berserk Meter | Hyperarmor shreds your poise | Track the meter and switch to dodge-only |
| Strafing in Wraith Form | Tracking projectiles still hit | Strafe perpendicular, not toward her |
| Panic-rolling the Aerial Slam | Costs stamina you need later | Jump or sidestep on the wind-up frame |

### Recovering From Breached State

If TieSha forces you into the **Breached state**, do not attempt to retaliate immediately, because your character will recover over roughly 4 seconds during which TieSha usually follows up with a thrust attack. Pre-emptively dodge toward her right shoulder during this window, because the thrust tracks more reliably from the left side and a well-timed roll here is essentially a free punish window. Mastering this micro-recovery is what separates a one-shot clear from a five-try slog, and the dedicated [Breached State recovery guide](/en/guides/breached-state-recovery/) covers the timing principles that apply to her follow-ups specifically. If you find yourself constantly getting caught on the thrust, the fix is almost always a recovery dodge input frame earlier than intuition suggests.

Paired with the unique Twin Fang daggers she also drops on first defeat, you have everything you need to comfortably face the next story checkpoint and to revisit earlier bosses for cleaner speedrun clears.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is TieSha the Frenzy a mandatory boss in Phantom Blade Zero?**

TieSha is an **optional boss** according to the official site, but skipping her locks you out of the Crimson Mantle accessory and the Twin Fang daggers. Many players report that her loot significantly eases the Huangxing the Sunken encounter that follows.

**What level should I be before fighting TieSha the Frenzy?**

Community testing suggests a recommended level range of around 45–55 with a fully upgraded medium-tier weapon. Going in under-levelled is technically possible with perfect parries, but her Wraith Form phase will two-shot most lower-vitality builds.

**Can you parry TieSha's Wraith Form projectiles?**

Yes, but the timing window is roughly 0.3 seconds and the projectiles have slightly different speeds depending on her distance. Using a **deflection art with extended parry frames** is the safest approach, because raw reaction time rarely covers all three projectile types on a first run.

**Does TieSha the Frenzy have any weaknesses to specific Sha-Chi arts?**

Fire-based Sha-Chi arts deal bonus damage during her Mad Cascade phase, because the hyperarmor animation has a fire-vulnerable hitbox tied to her Berserk Meter glow. Ice and lightning arts work fine but don't exploit the bonus multiplier.
