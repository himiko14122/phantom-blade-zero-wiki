## Understanding the Eight Endings in Phantom Blade Zero

Phantom Blade Zero ships with 8 distinct endings shaped by side quest completion and key story decisions. According to the [official site](https://pbz.s-game.com/), the game offers more ending variety than Elden Ring's 6, which is a bold claim for a Soulslike action title. Every ending branches from a few pivotal moments scattered across Acts II and III, and missing even one prerequisite silently reroutes you to a default conclusion that locks your save into a single cinematic.

Players chasing the **Phantom Blade Zero true ending** need to understand that the game does not warn you about irreversible choices. The system trusts you to back up saves manually or commit to a full playthrough, which makes pre-planning essential for completionists who want to avoid the "what if" regret after the final boss.

### How the Ending System Works

The ending triggers after the final boss encounter in the Crimson Pagoda. Once the cutscene begins, your save file locks into one of eight possible conclusions based on accumulated flags throughout the campaign. The community has mapped the underlying logic by cross-referencing save files, and the [Phantom Blade Zero Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4115450/Phantom_Blade_Zero/) confirms that NG+ allows exploring alternative paths without starting fresh.

### What Determines Your Ending

Five major story flags feed into the ending calculation engine, and each one operates on a binary or ternary state depending on the questline:

- **Red Wraith fate**: Kill or spare during the Burning Monastery encounter
- **Gong Meng rescue**: Complete his side chain before entering the Sunken Chapel
- **Liu's vendetta**: Resolve her grudge against the Iron Hand faction
- **Yao's ritual**: Find all four ritual talismans during Act II
- **Wei the Elder's blessing**: Accept or reject his final offer in Act III

The combination of these flags determines which cinematic plays after the final boss. Community testing on the [Phantom Blade Zero wiki page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phantom_Blade_Zero) confirms that the true ending requires at least four of these five flags in their "positive" state, with the fifth being optional and only altering the post-credits stinger.

## The Red Wraith Decision: The Most Important Story Branch

The Red Wraith encounter in Act II is widely reported by players as the moment that determines whether you can reach the true ending. She appears as a non-hostile NPC in the Burning Monastery after you defeat the Monastery Warden, then offers a dialogue branch that splits the story wide open based on a single timed prompt.

### Where to Find the Red Wraith

Progress through the Burning Monastery until you reach the Inner Sanctum. The Red Wraith kneels beside a fallen warrior near the altar; approach without locking on to avoid triggering her as an enemy. If you initiated combat earlier in the level, she may be hostile, which permanently blocks the **Phantom Blade Zero true ending** route on that save file.

| Step | Action | Consequence |
|------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Clear Monastery Warden | Opens Inner Sanctum gate |
| 2 | Approach Red Wraith unarmed | Triggers dialogue scene |
| 3 | Select "Stand Down" prompt | Preserves her as ally |
| 4 | Complete her side quest chain | Unlocks true ending flag |
| 5 | Skip or kill her early | Locks you into 3 alternative endings |

### The Three Conversation Outcomes

When the Red Wraith speaks, you have roughly 45 seconds of dialogue before the game auto-selects an aggressive option. The community has mapped three main outcomes based on extensive playthrough testing:

- **Spare and ally**: She joins your cause, grants access to the Sunken Chapel early, and her survival flag carries into the ending calculation. This is the required path for the true ending route.
- **Kill in self-defense**: She drops the Crimson Pendant, a key crafting material, but removes her flag from your save. You can still reach 6 of the 8 endings, but the true route becomes unreachable without a full NG+ reset.
- **Ignore and walk away**: Triggered by leaving the dialogue radius for more than five minutes. Community testing confirms this is functionally identical to killing her for ending purposes, though the Crimson Pendant remains unobtainable.

The "Stand Down" dialogue option only appears if you have not damaged any civilians in the Monastery prior to reaching the Inner Sanctum. Killing monks or destroying sacred objects silently removes this option before the prompt even renders, which is a frustrating design choice that catches many first-time players.

## Side Quest Requirements for the True Ending

Reaching the **Phantom Blade Zero true ending** demands completion of three optional side chains before the Sunken Chapel checkpoint. Skipping any one of them pushes you toward a darker conclusion, and the game gives no in-game tracker to remind you which quests are still active in your journal.

### The Three Required Side Chains

The three mandatory chains are spread across Acts I and II, and each one grants a specific ending flag when resolved:

- **Gong Meng's Rescue**: Locate Gong Meng in the Jade Forest catacombs, defeat his three captors, and escort him to the village healer. The quest concludes with a cutscene that grants the "Compassion" flag, which is required for the true ending.
- **Liu's Vendetta**: Find Liu in the Iron Hand camp, listen to her full backstory, and choose to either confront the Iron Hand captain or let her handle the grudge alone. The confrontational path grants the "Justice" flag.
- **Yao's Four Talismans**: Collect the four ritual talismans hidden in crypts across Act II. Each talisman adds to the "Ritual Complete" counter, and all four must be gathered before the Crimson Pagoda unlocks.

A common mistake new players make is treating these chains as optional content rather than core prerequisites. The [Phantom Blade Zero beginner guide](/en/guides/beginner-guide/) recommends treating every side quest as mandatory until proven otherwise, because the game rarely flags which chains feed into the ending calculation.

The Wei the Elder flag is technically optional for the **Phantom Blade Zero true ending**, but accepting his blessing changes the final boss's dialogue and unlocks an exclusive weapon skin in NG+. If you are chasing full completion, treat it as mandatory.

## NG+ Endings and Alternative Paths

The **Phantom Blade Zero NG+ endings** system lets you explore the seven "locked" conclusions without starting a fresh character from level one. NG+ carries over your level, gear, and most ending flags, but resets all side quest progress, which means you can re-run the Red Wraith encounter in a lower-stakes environment with better stats.

### What Carries Over in NG+

When you defeat the final boss and trigger any ending, the game offers a "New Journey" option that begins a fresh cycle with the following bonuses and resets:

- **Retained Level**: Your character level carries over, but enemy stats scale up by 20% per cycle to keep combat challenging.
- **Retained Gear**: All weapons, armor, and accessories stay in your inventory, including any Crimson Pendant or talisman duplicates.
- **Reset Quests**: Every side chain returns to its initial state, including the Red Wraith encounter and the three required flag quests.
- **New Ending Flags**: Two exclusive NG+ endings unlock after specific cycle milestones, both of which are unreachable in a standard playthrough.

| Cycle | New Content | Exclusive Ending |
|-------|-------------|------------------|
| NG+ (Cycle 1) | Harder bosses, new dialogue lines | None |
| NG+ (Cycle 2) | Red Wraith flashback quest | "The Forsaken Oath" |
| NG+ (Cycle 3) | Phantom Blade alternate form unlocked | None |
| NG+ (Cycle 4) | Full ending cinematic library access | "The Weaver's Truth" |
| NG+ (Cycle 5+) | Endgame bosses unlocked in base game | None |

### Efficient Strategy for All Eight Endings

Veteran completionists report that you can hit all 8 endings in roughly four playthroughs if you plan around the locked flags. The key is treating each NG+ cycle as a "choice run" focused on a different decision branch rather than replaying identical content:

- **Run 1**: Complete all three required side chains and spare the Red Wraith to trigger the **Phantom Blade Zero true ending**.
- **Run 2**: Kill the Red Wraith and refuse Liu's vendetta to unlock "The Burning Path."
- **Run 3**: Complete only Yao's ritual and abandon the other chains for "The Silent Blade."
- **Run 4**: NG+ Cycle 2 path for the Red Wraith flashback quest, ending in "The Forsaken Oath."

The [Phantom Blade Zero tips and tricks guide](/en/guides/tips-and-tricks/) covers save backup strategies that make this multi-run grind far less painful. Players on PC particularly benefit from manual save duplication before each major choice, while console players must rely on the single autosave slot and plan their first run more carefully to avoid backtracking.

## Step-by-Step Route to the True Ending

Here is the exact route to lock in the Phantom Blade Zero true ending on your first or second playthrough. Follow the steps in order, and back up your save before the Red Wraith encounter if you want a safety net against accidental mistakes.

1. Reach Act I, Chapter 4, and start Gong Meng's Rescue by talking to the village elder about the missing miner.
2. Clear the Jade Forest catacombs and defeat all three captors without letting Gong Meng's health drop below 50%.
3. Escort Gong Meng to the village healer and watch the full "Compassion" cutscene before leaving the area.
4. Enter Act II and begin Liu's Vendetta by speaking to Liu at the Iron Hand camp entrance after defeating the gate captain.
5. Choose to confront the Iron Hand captain personally and win the duel without Liu taking damage during the fight.
6. Collect all four of Yao's ritual talismans before entering the Sunken Chapel, which unlocks after defeating the Chapel Warden.
7. Reach the Burning Monastery, defeat the Monastery Warden, and approach the Red Wraith unarmed with no lock-on input.
8. Select "Stand Down" during the Red Wraith dialogue within the 45-second window, then complete her side quest chain.
9. Progress to Act III and accept Wei the Elder's blessing during his final offer in the Mountain Pavilion.
10. Enter the Crimson Pagoda, defeat the final boss without dying more than twice, and watch the true ending cinematic.

This route assumes you have a competent build for the Monastery Warden duel and the Iron Hand captain fight. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many endings does Phantom Blade Zero have?**

The game features 8 distinct endings in total, which is two more than Elden Ring's 6. According to the [official site](https://pbz.s-game.com/), only one qualifies as the canonical true ending, while the rest represent divergent story branches based on your side quest choices and the Red Wraith decision in Act II.

**Can you get the true ending on your first playthrough?**

Yes, the **Phantom Blade Zero true ending** is fully accessible on a first playthrough if you complete all three required side chains (Gong Meng, Liu, Yao) and spare the Red Wraith during the Burning Monastery encounter. Missing any one of these requirements silently reroutes you to an alternative conclusion, and the game gives no warning when you make a locking choice.

**Do NG+ endings require specific cycles?**

The two exclusive **Phantom Blade Zero NG+ endings** require specific cycle milestones to unlock. "The Forsaken Oath" appears on Cycle 2 after completing the Red Wraith flashback quest, while "The Weaver's Truth" plays on Cycle 4 once the full ending cinematic library is unlocked in your save metadata.

**What happens if you kill the Red Wraith?**

Killing the Red Wraith removes her flag from your save file and permanently blocks the true ending on that playthrough. You can still reach 6 of the 8 endings, but you would need to start a fresh NG+ cycle and replay her encounter unarmed with the "Stand Down" prompt selected to restore the flag.

**Is there a save file method to unlock all endings faster?**

Yes, on PC you can duplicate your save file before each major decision using the game's manual save folder located in the AppData directory. Console players can rely on the single autosave slot, which makes planning your first run more critical. Most players report hitting all 8 endings in roughly 25-30 hours with proper save management and the four-run strategy outlined above.
