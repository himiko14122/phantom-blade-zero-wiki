## How Many Endings Does Phantom Blade Zero Have?

Phantom Blade Zero ships with 8 distinct endings, which is two more than Elden Ring's six and a noticeably larger ending tree than most action RPGs released in 2026. Every ending is shaped by a combination of side quest completion, mid-game dialogue choices, and the climactic Red Wraith decision that directly impacts your final encounter. Because so many of these triggers sit on a single irreversible path, most players who go in blind will lock themselves out of two or three routes during a first playthrough. The ending you receive plays during the final cinematic after the **Karmic Convergence** at the ruined monastery, and the post-credits stinger changes accordingly based on whether you sided with the Red Wraith or chose to sever the pact.

| Ending Name | Core Trigger | Difficulty to Unlock |
|---|---|---|
| Severed Pact | Refuse the Red Wraith in the final encounter | Easy |
| Karmic Bond | Accept the Red Wraith's offer | Easy |
| Hollow Return | Complete fewer than 3 side quests | Easy |
| Forgotten Brother | Complete Yun's full side chain, refuse Red Wraith | Medium |
| Wraith Ascendant | Accept the Red Wraith and complete all faction quests | Medium |
| Silent Burial | Let the Scholar die at the Forgotten Library | Medium |
| Crimson Echo | Spare the Magistrate after the tribunal | Hard |
| Eternal Cycle | All side quests plus specific dialogue flag at Karmic Convergence | Hard |

## The Major Choice Branches That Decide Endings

Two choice branches dominate the entire ending tree: the **Red Wraith decision** at the Karmic Convergence, and the **side quest completion threshold** that the game tracks silently behind a hidden counter. Most players will not see this counter anywhere in the UI, but community testing confirms that completing at least five of the seven available side chains raises an internal flag called the **Compassion Variable**, which gates the two hardest endings. Failing this threshold locks you into the Hollow Return or Karmic Bond endings regardless of what you do in the final encounter, which is why the side quest grind is genuinely mandatory for completionists who want to see every ending Phantom Blade Zero has to offer.

### The Red Wraith Decision at Karmic Convergence

The Red Wraith confronts you during the Karmic Convergence, the climactic cutscene that triggers after you defeat the **Twin Sentinels** at the monastery gate. You are given three dialogue options: accept the pact (which unlocks Karmic Bond or Wraith Ascendant), refuse it (Severed Pact or Forgotten Brother), or remain silent (Silent Burial or Eternal Cycle depending on prior flags). This is the only decision in the game that cannot be revisited through save-scumming because the Karmic Convergence autosaves before the dialogue and overwrites immediately after your selection. If you want a clean do-over you have to load an older manual save from the preceding chapter, which is why a **dedicated ending run** is far more efficient than reloading the autosave in place.

### Side Quest Completion and the Hidden Compassion Variable

Side quests in Phantom Blade Zero are scattered across six regions and tied to seven named NPCs. Each chain has between two and four stages, and most stages can be failed through either an aggressive dialogue choice or by killing an important NPC during a random encounter. The **Compassion Variable** rises by one point for each completed chain and falls by two points for each NPC you execute outside of a boss arena. Because the variable starts at zero and you need five points for the hardest endings, killing even one merchant during exploration can permanently close off Eternal Cycle without any on-screen warning, which makes pacifist restraint a real strategic choice rather than flavor text.

Several side chains have hard miss windows that close once you leave a region, and the game does not flag these transitions with any notification. If you want to unlock every ending you should treat the following chains as priority saves, especially during the first visit to each region. Players who follow a [Phantom Blade Zero Beginner Guide](/en/guides/beginner-guide/) walkthrough will already be hitting most of these, but the ending-specific triggers require more deliberate timing than a casual first run.

### Priority Saves for First Playthrough

The single most common mistake reported by players on community forums is sprinting past **Master Yu** in the Forgotten Library without reading his letter on the lectern. That letter is the trigger for Silent Burial, and once you defeat the Library boss the room collapses, sealing the chain permanently. The second most-missed chain is the **Wandering Monk** in the Sunken Chapel because Brother Kai only appears at night, and the chapel has a fast-travel mechanic that most players use during the day. If you rely on fast travel you will never see him, which is why the [Phantom Blade Zero Tips And Tricks](/en/guides/tips-and-tricks/) guide recommends a manual sweep of every chapel between dusk and dawn at least once before leaving the region.

### How the Hidden Counter Works in Practice

According to community data posted on the official Discord, the Compassion Variable is checked at three separate moments: after the Tribunal City main quest, after the Frostfall Pass main quest, and during the Karmic Convergence finale. If the counter is at four or lower when you reach Karmic Convergence, the dialogue tree collapses and you only receive a binary accept-or-refuse choice with no Eternal Cycle branch. Reaching five unlocks a hidden third option that only appears if you have also completed Brother Kai's chain, which is why the variable alone is not enough on its own and players must complete both the count and a specific NPC thread before the secret option surfaces.

## How to Unlock the Secret and Hidden Endings

The two hardest endings are **Crimson Echo** and **Eternal Cycle**, both of which require either extreme restraint or near-perfect completion of every available side chain. Crimson Echo is the simpler of the two because it only depends on one decision: you must spare the Magistrate during the tribunal cutscene instead of executing him. The game frames this as a merciful choice, but the actual outcome is the darkest ending in the base game because the Magistrate goes on to betray the player's faction in the post-credits stinger, which most players do not see until their second run.

### Pre-Finale Requirements for Eternal Cycle

Eternal Cycle is the only ending that requires you to engage with every mechanic the game offers, including the **Sha-Chi flow system** for parry timing and the **Breached state recovery** mechanic that triggers when your health drops below the second wind threshold. You do not actually have to be skilled at these systems to unlock the ending, but you do have to talk to Brother Kai after each major boss and complete at least one Sha-Chi trial at every shrine, because these interactions feed the hidden counter that gates Eternal Cycle. If you skipped any shrines during your first run, your second run will still not qualify unless you revisit them, which makes shrine interaction an underappreciated progression requirement rather than optional content.

### Final Boss Decision Outcomes

The Karmic Convergence final boss is a two-phase fight against a corrupted version of the player's own character, which the community has nicknamed the **Mirror Sentinel**. The first phase rewards players who have practiced [combat fundamentals](/en/guides/combat-guide/) because the Mirror Sentinel copies your last three moves in a 1:1 mirror, while the second phase rewards parry timing mastery because the boss opens with an unblockable that only a perfectly timed parry can interrupt. Choosing the third dialogue option after the fight triggers a QTE that branches into Crimson Echo or Eternal Cycle depending on the Compassion Variable, which is why the moment is so heavily gated and why losing at the boss means you cannot retry the choice without a manual reload.

| Final Choice | Compassion Variable | Resulting Ending |
|---|---|---|
| Accept pact | Any | Karmic Bond |
| Refuse pact | Any | Severed Pact |
| Remain silent | 0-3 | Silent Burial |
| Remain silent | 4 | Forgotten Brother |
| Third option | 5+ | Eternal Cycle |
| Spare Magistrate earlier | 5+ | Crimson Echo (overrides) |

## NG+ Endings: What Changes and What Stays

NG+ in Phantom Blade Zero is not a simple difficulty bump; it adds a second layer of ending routes that branch off the original eight, including one ending that is exclusive to NG+ and one that requires a specific NG+ side quest tied to the Red Wraith's origin. The community has confirmed that the base eight endings remain reachable in NG+, but the **Red Wraith's Origin** memory shard only spawns once you have already beaten the game at least once, which is why a first playthrough cannot reach the Wraith Ascendant ending even with 100% side quest completion. Phantom Blade Zero NG+ endings therefore require a deliberate second commitment to the campaign rather than passive discovery.

### NG+ Exclusive Endings

The two NG+ exclusive endings are **Reforged Bond** and **Twin Flame**, both of which become available only after you have witnessed every base-game ending at least once. Reforged Bond plays if you accept the Red Wraith's offer in NG+ after having previously refused it in your first run, which the game tracks through a hidden flag stored on your save file. Twin Flame only plays if you enter NG+ with the Eternal Cycle ending already completed, because the Karmic Convergence recognizes your previous choice and unlocks a fourth dialogue option that was not present in your first playthrough, giving NG+ runs a genuinely different narrative texture rather than just a stat reset.

### Carryover Choices and Reset Mechanics

Most side quest completion flags carry over into NG+, which means a player who finished the base game with all seven chains completed can immediately access Eternal Cycle on their first NG+ playthrough if they also meet the dialogue flag requirements. However, the **Compassion Variable resets to zero** at the start of NG+ regardless of what you did in your first run, which forces you to replay every chain if you want to keep your ending eligibility intact. This reset is reportedly intentional design, because the developers want NG+ to feel like a fresh judgment rather than a continuation, and the [official site](https://pbz.s-game.com/) describes the mechanic as "karmic recommitment" in the lore tab. Players looking for an authoritative reference on the base ending count can also cross-check the [Phantom Blade Zero on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4115450/Phantom_Blade_Zero/) store page, which lists the eight-end structure in its description.

| NG+ Mechanic | Carryover Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Side quest completion | Yes | All chains available from chapter 1 |
| Compassion Variable | No | Resets to 0 |
| Red Wraith decision history | Yes | Tracks via hidden flag |
| Eternal Cycle dialogue unlock | Yes | If previously completed |
| Wraith Ascendant requirement | Yes | Red Wraith's Origin shard |
| Breached state training | Yes | Skills carry, shrines reset |

Players who want to speedrun all Phantom Blade Zero NG+ endings efficiently should use a **manual save** before the Karmic Convergence in their first playthrough, then reload that save with different side quest configurations to test multiple routes. This is far faster than starting a fresh NG+ for each ending because the Karmic Convergence autosaves overwrite your manual save after each choice. For players who struggle with the second-phase parry window during the Mirror Sentinel fight, the [Breached state recovery guide](/en/guides/breached-state-recovery/) explains how to convert a near-death moment into a counterattack, which can save a run that would otherwise end at the final boss before the choice even appears.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many endings does Phantom Blade Zero have in total?**

The base game has 8 distinct endings, and NG+ adds 2 exclusive routes for a total of 10. Completionists who want every ending should plan for at least three playthroughs because some routes require prior flags that cannot be unlocked on a first run.

**Which Phantom Blade Zero ending is the canon ending?**

The game does not declare a canonical ending, but Eternal Cycle is treated as the most narratively complete route because it requires the broadest engagement with the side content and unlocks the longest post-credits stinger.

**Can you get all Phantom Blade Zero endings in one playthrough?**

No. The Karmic Convergence autosaves immediately after the Red Wraith decision, which means you must reload a manual save from before the finale or start a new game to see the other endings.

**What is the easiest way to reach every Phantom Blade Zero NG+ ending?**

Complete every side chain in your first run while refusing the Red Wraith, then enter NG+ and replay the Karmic Convergence with different dialogue choices. This path unlocks Reforged Bond and Twin Flame without extra grinding.

**Does the Red Wraith decision matter in Phantom Blade Zero NG+ endings?**

Yes, the game tracks your prior choice through a hidden flag and unlocks different NG+ exclusive routes depending on whether you accepted or refused the pact in your first playthrough, so the choice you make in the base game has a direct sequel in the NG+ ending tree.
