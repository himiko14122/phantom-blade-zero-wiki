The wuxia-inflected RPG packs more divergent finales than most modern action titles, so understanding **the full ending conditions matrix** separates completionists from players who replay the same finale twice without realizing why. The [Phantom Blade Zero Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4115450/Phantom_Blade_Zero/) confirms eight distinct conclusions shaped by side quest completion and pivotal faction decisions, a count that already outpaces Elden Ring's six routes and Sekiro's four. If you want every narrative branch without burning eighty hours per cycle, this breakdown of the decision gates, hidden triggers, and how **Phantom Blade Zero NG+ endings** layer on top of the base game will keep your playthrough efficient.

## The Eight Finale Routes at a Glance

Eight conclusions sound intimidating until you notice that the underlying architecture is more linear than it appears. According to the [official Phantom Blade Zero site](https://pbz.s-game.com/), the route you trigger depends on a small handful of binary choices clustered around the Red Wraith recruitment, the Jade Blossom tea house subplot, and the Order of Falling Suns vault. Each finale swaps two cinematic bosses, one secret area, and a single post-credits stinger, which means every flag you flip creates visible consequences rather than label-only variations.

| # | Ending Name | Primary Trigger | Reachable in First Run? |
|---|-------------|-----------------|--------------------------|
| 1 | Silent Blade | Refuse Red Wraith at Black Crane Bridge | Yes (Default) |
| 2 | Blood Oath | Accept Red Wraith, complete tea house arc | Yes |

| 4 | Ashen Throne | Betray Red Wraith in chapter 8 | Yes |
| 5 | Crimson Lantern | Complete Lantern Keeper side quest chain | Yes |

The **Dual Eclipse** route is unique because the Memory Shard hunt can be finished in your first run, but the **Eternal Cycle** branch is impossible without at least two New Game Plus loops. That layering, where certain finales only become reachable after you have already seen the others, is what gives the broader **Phantom Blade Zero NG+ endings** ladder its long-tail value and why the community treats the final route as a true capstone.

## How Ending Conditions Are Calculated

Behind the curtain, every choice routes through a hidden decision matrix the developers at S-Game have publicly described as a "branching-but-bounded" structure, summarized on the [Phantom Blade Zero Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phantom_Blade_Zero). The matrix watches for eight flags across three categories: allegiance toggles, side-quest completion, and a secret counter that tracks how many Memory Shards you have slotted into your keepsake box. All flags resolve at the moment chapter 9 begins, which is why mid-game saves act as effective branch points.

### Allegiance Flags

The allegiance system watches four NPCs you can save, spare, or execute. Allegiance flags flip permanently the moment you resolve their personal quest, and later backtracking cannot undo them without returning to an older save file. Because the flags persist across NG+ tiers, careful players intentionally tank one allegiance to keep the alternate route accessible later.

### Completion Flags

Side-quest chains contribute a single binary flag each. Missing a quest such as the **Lantern Keeper** chain automatically locks out the Crimson Lantern finale, while finishing it flips a flag for the post-credits scene regardless of which faction you sided with earlier. The completion flags do not overwrite one another, which means you can carry multiple completion achievements into the same finale if you plan ahead.

| Flag Type | Example Trigger | Reversible? | Resolved At |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------|--------------|
| Allegiance | Spare Magistrate Luo (Ch.6) | No | Chapter 6 trigger |
| Completion | Lantern Keeper chain (Ch.4-7) | No | Chapter 7 closure |
| Hidden Counter | 12 Memory Shards slotted | Save-scum only | Chapter 9 entry |

This table is the heart of the **Phantom Blade Zero ending conditions** system, because it shows that a single missed NPC, whether caused by a stealth-kill glitch or a skipped dialogue option, can collapse four potential routes into the default Silent Blade finale. If you are still learning the early-game combat fundamentals, the [Phantom Blade Zero Beginner Guide](/en/guides/beginner-guide/) is a safer starting reference before committing to a flag-heavy run.

## Critical Decision Points You Cannot Undo

Every finale traces back to one of five pivotal scenes, and missing any of them creates a soft lock that no amount of late-game farming can repair. The community has mapped these triggers in extensive detail, but the practical breakdown reduces to a handful of tight timing windows and a couple of hidden dialogue prompts that only fire under specific weather or time-of-day conditions.

### The Red Wraith Recruitment

In chapter 3, the Red Wraith appears at Black Crane Bridge offering a recruitment pact. Players who accept unlock the **Blood Oath** and Wraith Ascendant paths, while those who refuse default toward Silent Blade or Gilded Cage. Acceptance is a one-way dial because the Wraith joins your party and remains present through the chapter 8 betrayal decision, which is why veteran players recommend backing up the save the moment the bridge dialogue triggers.

### The Magistrate Luo Spare

At the end of chapter 6, Magistrate Luo surrenders after the duel in the Burning Archive. Choosing to spare him unlocks **Gilded Cage** and **Wraith Ascendant** simultaneously, while executing him locks those two and feeds the Ashen Throne branch. Because both branches share prerequisite allegiance flags, it is mathematically impossible to see both in one playthrough, which is precisely why first-time players often miss one of the routes.

| Decision Point | Chapter | Window | Routes Enabled |
|----------------|---------|--------|----------------|
| Red Wraith recruitment | 3 | Single dialogue | Blood Oath, Wraith Ascendant |
| Spare Magistrate Luo | 6 | End of duel | Gilded Cage, Wraith Ascendant |
| Betray Red Wraith | 8 | Cutscene choice | Ashen Throne, Wraith Ascendant |
| Memory Shard count | 1-9 | Open-world | Dual Eclipse, Eternal Cycle |
| Lantern Keeper chain | 4-7 | Quest chain | Crimson Lantern, Eternal Cycle |

The interaction table above shows why planning matters early. Three of these gates feed the same **Wraith Ascendant** finale, so a single run can only ever reach that route through one path, encouraging a multi-cycle strategy that defines the **Phantom Blade Zero NG+ endings** ladder.

## NG+ Cycles and Exclusive Routes

Once you finish any finale, the New Game Plus selector unlocks at the main menu and you can carry over selected progression. According to community testing as of early 2026, NG+ introduces tougher enemy modifiers, a redesigned boss at Black Crane Bridge, and two previously unreachable routes gated behind higher difficulty tiers. The carry-over rules are strict enough that currency resets while weapon mastery persists, which shifts the meta toward parry-first playstyles.

### What Carries Over

You keep your weapon mastery, half your collected currency, and any Memory Shards already slotted into your keepsake box, but consumables, save crystals, and certain key items reset. Combat veterans looking to optimize the early NG+ hours can refer to the [Phantom Blade Zero Combat Guide](/en/guides/combat-guide/) for parry timing adjustments needed against the redesigned bridge boss, whose attack cadence shifts noticeably on the second cycle.

### NG-Exclusive Routes

Reaching the Eternal Cycle finale requires NG+2 with every allegiance flag set to its most extreme value, a run that effectively demands you have already seen Silent Blade, Blood Oath, and Gilded Cage at least once. Because of this prerequisite chain, Eternal Cycle is the only finale in the broader **Phantom Blade Zero NG+ endings** ladder that closes the achievement tree and awards the platinum trophy.

| NG Tier | New Element | New Ending Accessible |
|---------|-------------|------------------------|
| NG+1 | Veteran mobs, redesigned bridge boss | Dual Eclipse becomes solo-friendly |
| NG+2 | Elite modifiers, hidden courtyard | Eternal Cycle unlocks |
| NG+3 | Permadeath toggle | Cosmetic rewards only |

The cosmetic row in the table explains why some fans recommend stopping at NG+2. Beyond that tier you are grinding for a different katana skin rather than new narrative, which means the practical boundary for completionists is NG+2 rather than NG+3.

## Strategy for Unlocking All Eight Endings

Eight routes spread across at least two NG+ cycles is a lot of ground to cover, but a deliberate save rotation can cut that to roughly four controlled playthroughs. The trick is to recognize which decisions are mutually exclusive and which ride the same flag rails, then lean into the saves that produce the most finals per file.

### Save-Scumming Approach

Slot one manual save at the start of chapter 3, another at chapter 6, and a third at chapter 8. Loading from those checkpoints lets you branch through six of the eight finales without replaying tutorial content. This technique is faster than the traditional two-character method used in Souls-likes because the chapter checkpoints autosave into distinct files, letting you toggle routes with a single menu swap.

### Recommended Play Order

Most community-tested routes suggest finishing Silent Blade first as your baseline run, then reloading chapter 3 to chase Blood Oath, and finally jumping into NG+1 for Dual Eclipse. If your parry timing is shaky on the NG+ bridge encounter, brush up on the [Phantom Blade Zero How To Parry](/en/guides/how-to-parry/) walkthrough before tackling the redesigned boss. For players who lean on mobility instead of blocks, the companion [Phantom Blade Zero How To Dodge](/en/guides/how-to-dodge/) article pairs well with the run plan below.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many endings does Phantom Blade Zero have in total?**

The game ships with eight distinct conclusions. Seven are reachable in your first playthrough, while the eighth requires NG+2 and every allegiance flag pushed to its limit.

**Do Phantom Blade Zero NG+ endings add new story content?**

Yes. NG+1 adds a redesigned mid-game boss and one bonus cinematic, and NG+2 unlocks the courtyard that gates the Eternal Cycle finale alongside new dialogue variants.

**Can I reach multiple finales in a single playthrough?**

You can reach exactly one finale per playthrough, but manual saves at chapters 3, 6, and 8 let you swap between six of the eight without restarting from the title screen.

**What happens if I skip a side quest by accident?**

Skipping the Lantern Keeper chain, the Red Wraith recruitment, or the Memory Shard collection permanently locks out one or more finales. Reload the oldest available save to recover the missing flag before chapter 9 triggers the resolution check.

**Which ending is considered canon?**

S-Game has not confirmed a canon route, though community consensus treats Silent Blade as the default starting point because its flag state matches the marketing trailer most closely.
