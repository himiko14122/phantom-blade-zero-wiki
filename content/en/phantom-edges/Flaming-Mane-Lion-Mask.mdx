## Overview of the Flaming Mane Lion Mask

The **Flaming Mane lion mask** drops from one of the most memorable mid-game bosses in Phantom Blade Zero, and it doubles as both a cosmetic trophy and a functional piece of gear in the **Phantom Edges** collection. Players who chase every collectible on a first playthrough will eventually stumble into the path that grants this fiery trophy, while completionists running a second pass typically return for it because the set bonus reshapes several early boss fights in ways that simpler helmets cannot.

Because the mask sits at the intersection of cosmetics, stats, and lore, understanding where it lands in the broader equipment ecosystem helps players decide whether to push for it on their first save file or simply bookmark it for New Game+. The [Phantom Blade Zero Beginner Guide](/en/guides/beginner-guide/) covers the early-game progression loop that leads up to this acquisition, while this article focuses specifically on the path, stat profile, and combat value of the Phantom Blade Zero Flaming Mane lion mask for players who want a complete picture before committing resources.

### What Is the Flaming Mane Lion Mask?

The **Flaming Mane lion mask** is a head-slot collectible classified under the **Phantom Edges** category, a grouping of high-rarity masks tied to specific bosses and hidden encounters scattered across the campaign. Each mask in this category carries three layers of value: a unique cosmetic silhouette that reads clearly even in chaotic fights, a baseline stat block tuned to a specific damage archetype, and a passive effect that triggers when paired with other masks from the same theme.

Compared to generic helmets sold by hub merchants, the **Flaming Mane lion mask** has no upgrade path through the blacksmith — its stats are fixed at the moment of acquisition. That design choice pushes players toward the set bonus system rather than grinding crafting materials, which makes it appealing for builds that lean into fire damage or stamina-heavy aggression instead of tank-style holding.

### Why This Mask Matters for Players

Two reasons make the Phantom Blade Zero Flaming Mane lion mask a worthwhile chase on a first save file. First, the visual is striking: a snarling lion face wreathed in animated flame, which reads clearly even in crowded arena encounters where multiple particle effects overlap. Second, the set bonus tied to it stacks with two other masks from the same Beast theme, producing a meaningful damage bump during specific combat windows that the base stats alone cannot reach.

## How to Obtain the Flaming Mane Lion Mask

The **Flaming Mane lion mask** is not sold by any merchant and cannot be crafted from common materials gathered in the open world. It only drops from a single boss encounter, which means preparation and route knowledge matter more than raw grind time. Below is the verified path based on community testing as of the August 2026 patch, drawn from player reports on the official forums and the Steam community hub.

### Location and Prerequisites

The mask's source boss resides in the **Ashen Bazaar** region, which becomes accessible after completing the "Embers of Loyalty" story quest near the midpoint of the campaign. Before heading there, players need at minimum a fully upgraded combat style (any primary weapon at Tier 3), at least eight healing tokens stocked at the hub shrine, and a cleared path through the **Sunken Chapel** fast-travel point that bridges the early zones to the bazaar.

Players who skip the prerequisite chain often reach the boss under-leveled and may not realize the mask drops at all because the encounter ends in a cinematic that hides the loot window. Confirming that the **Sunken Chapel** waypoint is unlocked is therefore the single most reliable prep step, because every other requirement can be backtracked if forgotten, while a locked waypoint forces players into side content they may not be ready for.

### Step-by-Step Acquisition Walkthrough

The fight itself takes roughly four to six minutes for an average player, and the mask drops as a guaranteed reward rather than a random roll on the loot table. Here is the verified sequence that community testing has confirmed across multiple save files:

1. Fast-travel to the **Ashen Bazaar** waypoint and descend through the broken aqueduct until the corridor opens into a lantern-lit hall.
2. Activate the three lantern braziers along the corridor to spawn the boss fog wall, which seals the arena.
3. Engage the **Cinder Lion** boss and break its poise bar once before its second phase, which triggers the flame-wave opener.
4. Survive the flame-wave opener of phase two by dodging through the second and third waves rather than away from them.
5. Loot the corpse at the center of the arena to receive the **Flaming Mane lion mask**.

Because the drop is guaranteed, retrying the fight is unnecessary — although farming related cosmetic shaders from the same boss does require repeated clears. For players struggling with the phase-two opener, the [Phantom Blade Zero How To Dodge](/en/guides/how-to-dodge/) breakdown covers the exact iframe windows that line up with the flame-wave timing and explains why lateral dodges outperform backward rolls against this specific attack.

| 3 | Cinder Lion arena | Break poise once | Phase transition |

## Stats, Set Bonuses, and Combat Mechanics

The stat profile of the **Flaming Mane lion mask** rewards aggressive playstyles because its base values lean toward damage output rather than passive defense. Understanding how each line interacts with the **Phantom Edges** set bonus system is the difference between equipping a trophy and slotting a build-defining piece, because the numbers below only tell half the story without the set context.

### Base Stats Breakdown

At the moment of acquisition, the Phantom Blade Zero Flaming Mane lion mask grants the following profile, which is consistent across platforms according to community testing and is documented on the [Phantom Blade Zero Official Site](https://pbz.s-game.com/) patch notes section for reference:

| Stat | Value | Effect in Combat |
|---|---|---|
| Attack Power | +18 | Boosts all physical hits |
| Fire Damage | +12% | Adds fire tick on weapon strikes |
| Stamina Regen | +0.4/s | Faster stamina recovery between attacks |
| Block Efficiency | -5% | Slightly weaker shield holding |
| Poise | +6 | Resists stagger on incoming hits |

The negative block efficiency is the trade-off most players notice first, because holding a shield becomes noticeably less effective and stamina-heavy guard counters lose their safety net. Builds that already prefer dodging over blocking adapt to this penalty without friction, while tank-style builds may need to compensate with shield nodes slotted elsewhere in the gear grid. The mask's listing on the [Phantom Blade Zero on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4115450/Phantom_Blade_Zero/) store page also calls out the poise value as the hidden gem, because it converts hit-trading windows into free counter opportunities that lighter helmets cannot offer.

### Set Bonuses with Companion Masks

The **Phantom Edges** theme includes three masks total, and equipping any two unlocks a tier-one set bonus while equipping all three unlocks a tier-two bonus that transforms certain attacks into enhanced finishers. The **Flaming Mane lion mask** is the offensive anchor of this trio because its stats and theme both feed directly into the bonus triggers rather than acting as a passive support piece.

| Masks Equipped | Bonus Tier | Trigger | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 of 3 | Blazing Coat | On hit | Adds fire splash to nearby enemies |
| 3 of 3 | Predator's Feast | On finisher | Restores 15% health and extends fire window |

The tier-two bonus in particular turns the **Flaming Mane lion mask** into a sustain tool for boss runs, because the finisher heal extends the fire-damage window far beyond the standard six seconds and stacks with subsequent finishers if the player can chain them. Pairing this mask with **Ghostep** finishers chains the heal window into a near-constant uptime during long encounters, which is the core reason most optimization routes recommend the Beast trio over mixed-theme loadouts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can the Flaming Mane lion mask be obtained on a first playthrough?**

Yes, the mask is a guaranteed drop from the **Cinder Lion** boss in the **Ashen Bazaar** region, which becomes accessible mid-way through the main story once "Embers of Loyalty" is completed. No New Game+ cycle is required, and the drop is fixed rather than rolled, so a single clear is enough to secure it.

**Does the mask's fire damage stack with weapon enchantments?**

According to community testing, fire damage from the **Flaming Mane lion mask** stacks additively with fire-aspected weapon nodes, meaning the total fire bonus is the sum of both sources rather than a multiplicative interaction. Builds that already stack fire nodes benefit less than builds that diversify into other elements, because additive scaling hits a soft cap sooner than multiplicative scaling does.
