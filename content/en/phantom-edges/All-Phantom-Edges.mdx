## What Are Phantom Edges in Phantom Blade Zero

**Phantom Edges** are the secondary weapon slot in Phantom Blade Zero, designed to give your main sword a tactical exit door when the situation breaks down. According to the [Phantom Blade Zero on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4115450/Phantom_Blade_Zero/) store page, every **Phantom Edge** has a limited number of uses per encounter, meaning the weapon you bring into a boss fight vanishes the moment its charges run out.

That limitation is the entire point of the system. Players who treat their Edge like a free panic button burn through their kit in two boss phases, while players who map each tool to a specific combat situation keep pressure on targets across the whole fight. The system also rewards preparation because boss drops that unlock new Edges are gated behind [combat fundamentals](/en/guides/combat-guide/) that you can only learn through repeated runs.

### How the Charge System Works

Each **Phantom Edge** comes with a fixed number of charges that refill automatically between encounters but never during a single fight. Using the **Flaming Mane** lion mask to fire a flame burst costs one charge, while unleashing the **Man-Cutter** halberd's heavy combo eats two or three charges depending on the finisher you choose. Once the counter hits zero, the Edge locks itself out, and you fall back to your main weapon until the next encounter.

Players report that some Edges have hidden cooldown windows between activations, which prevents spam during stagger windows. The system also interacts with the **Breached state**, since a staggered enemy can take amplified damage from a fully charged Edge attack. For more on capitalizing on that opening, our [Breached state recovery guide](/en/guides/breached-state-recovery/) walks through the timing chain in detail.

## Combat Strategy With Limited-Use Edges

Managing a finite charge pool during a multi-phase boss is the real skill behind the **all Phantom Edges** system. New players tend to fire off their Edge the moment the boss enters a vulnerable animation, which wastes a charge that could have been saved for a clutch second phase. Veterans instead front-load their basic sword combos, save the Edge for the moment the boss shifts attack patterns, and use the Edge only when the **Breached state** is fully triggered.

If you want a deeper breakdown of parry and dodge timing, our [how to parry](/en/guides/how-to-parry/) and [how to dodge](/en/guides/how-to-dodge/) resources pair well with the charge management approach below.

### When to Burn Your Charges

The safest moment to spend a charge is during a boss recovery animation that lasts at least two seconds, because that window is long enough for both the Edge activation and a follow-up main weapon combo. Burning a charge during a half-second stagger, by contrast, leaves you exposed for the counter swing that follows.

| Situation | Recommended Edge Use | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Boss recovery after Phase 2 opener | Spend 1-2 charges | Longest stagger window |
| Trash mob swarm before miniboss | Spend 1 charge on AoE | Clear space, save melee |
| Phase 3 desperation attack | Save for clutch parry | Low charge, high pressure |
| Optional boss first attempt | Hold for learning phase | Use basic sword to study patterns |

The general rule is to enter every boss with at least one charge in reserve, because the third phase of most encounters includes a scripted sequence where your main weapon is locked out. A well-timed **Flaming Mane** charge at that moment often decides the run.

## Phantom Edges vs Main Weapons

The main weapon and the **Phantom Edge** are not competing systems, they are layered systems. The main weapon defines your combos, mobility, and stamina economy across the entire run, while the Edge handles the specific problems that your main weapon cannot solve. A katana main, for example, can pressure stagger windows beautifully but struggles against shielded knights, which is exactly the gap the **Shattered Coil** ranged Edge fills.

| Role | Main Weapon | Phantom Edge |
|---|---|---|
| Stamina Cost | Variable, regenerates | Flat per use, no regen in fight |
| Combo Length | 3-6 hits | 1-3 hits |
| Best Window | Extended neutral | Punish windows |
| Risk Level | Manageable | High if wasted |
| Damage Type | Slash, thrust, blunt | Mixed, often elemental |
| Replaceable? | Between encounters | Fixed per fight |

Players who skip the Edge entirely lose a layer of tactical depth that the developers clearly intended, because several boss patterns are tuned around forcing the player to switch tools. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many Phantom Edges are there in Phantom Blade Zero?**

The current build ships with **20+ Phantom Edges** across ranged, melee, and heavy weapon classes, and datamined files suggest several more are in development for future content patches.

**What is the best Phantom Edge for beginners?**

The **Man-Cutter halberd** is the safest first Edge because it carries five charges per encounter, deals high stagger damage, and forgives mistimed swings better than heavier options like the **Bashpole**.

**Where do I farm the Bashpole warhammer?**

The **Bashpole** drops exclusively from the **Coppermaul** boss on the critical path, and you will need a level 12-15 character plus a main weapon that can pressure his shield phase consistently.

**Do Phantom Edge charges carry between encounters?**

No, charges refill automatically at the start of every new encounter, but they do not regenerate during a single fight, so plan your usage around boss phases rather than wave-by-wave combat.

**Can I use more than one Phantom Edge in a single fight?**

You can swap Edges from your inventory mid-encounter, but each swap consumes a small amount of time and leaves you briefly vulnerable, so most players stick to one Edge per major boss.

**Are Phantom Edge drops guaranteed from bosses?**

Yes, boss-dropped Edges are guaranteed on first clear, and you can replay any boss in the Memorial Hall to collect alternate Edge variants for build experimentation.
