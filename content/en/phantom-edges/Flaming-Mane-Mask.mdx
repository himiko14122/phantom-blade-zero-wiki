## Flaming Mane Mask Phantom Edge in Phantom Blade Zero: Fire Damage & Abilities

Among the 20-plus Phantom Edges at Soul’s disposal, few command the battlefield with the sheer spectacle of the **Flaming Mane Mask**. This lion-faced apparatus doesn’t just intimidate—it engulfs enemies in roaring flames, applying persistent burn damage that continues ticking long after the initial blast. Whether you’re crowd-controlling a group of lesser foes or stacking damage-over-time effects on a stubborn boss like Coppermaul, the Flaming Mane earns its slot as one of the most versatile off-hand tools in *Phantom Blade Zero*.

This guide breaks down exactly how the Flaming Mane Mask works, when to deploy it, which weapons it pairs with best, and how to build an entire playstyle around its searing flames.

---

## What the Flaming Mane Mask Does

The Flaming Mane Mask is a Phantom Edge—a secondary weapon or gadget that Soul can deploy independently of his primary melee weapon. Visually, it manifests as an ornate bronze lion mask that materializes in front of Soul and exhales a wide cone of fire across the battlefield. The attack covers a generous area in front of you, making it excellent for hitting multiple enemies simultaneously.

### Core Mechanics

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Damage Type** | Fire (elemental) |
| **Status Effect** | Burn — deals damage over 8 seconds |
| **Sha-Chi Cost** | Moderate (approximately 15-20% of a full bar) |
| **Cooldown** | ~12 seconds at base, reducible via Skill Tree |
| **Range** | Medium cone, roughly 5 meters deep |
| **Best Against** | Flesh enemies, grouped mobs, enemies weak to fire |

The Flaming Mane doesn’t require ammunition or consumables. It runs purely on **Sha-Chi**, the same combat resource you spend on heavy attacks and parries. This means every activation is a tactical choice—do you save Sha-Chi for a Power Surge, or do you burn it (literally) to apply pressure from range?

---

## Burn Status Effect: How Fire Damage Works

The burn status is what elevates the Flaming Mane from a simple damage tool into a strategic centerpiece. Here’s how it functions:

- **Application**: Any enemy caught in the Flaming Mane’s cone receives one stack of burn (some larger enemies can receive multiple stacks if hit by overlapping flame segments).
- **Duration**: Burn ticks every second for 8 seconds at base.
- **Damage per Tick**: Scales with your weapon’s fire damage bonus and relevant Skill Tree nodes.
- **Stacking**: Up to 3 burn stacks can accumulate on a single target. Each stack operates independently, so 3 stacks means triple the tick damage.
- **Interaction with Breached State**: Enemies in the Breached state (Sha-Chi depleted) take 25% increased burn damage. This creates a devastating feedback loop—open with Flaming Mane, then break their guard to amplify the flames already eating at them.

Burn damage ignores armor, making it particularly effective against heavily plated enemies who shrug off physical hits. Bosses like **TieSha the Frenzy** and **Huangxing the Sunken**, who rely on thick defenses, become substantially more manageable when a burn build is chipping away at their health bar regardless of your melee uptime.

## Sha-Chi Cost and Cooldown Management

Understanding the resource economy of the Flaming Mane is critical to using it effectively. At moderate Sha-Chi cost, you can fire it roughly every 12 seconds—but aggressive players will want to invest in the Phantom Edge branch of the Skill Tree to reduce both cost and cooldown.

### Recommended Skill Tree Investments

- **Phantom Efficiency I/II**: Reduces Sha-Chi cost of all Phantom Edges by 10/20%.
- **Lion’s Breath**: Specifically reduces Flaming Mane cooldown by 3 seconds.
- **Residual Embers**: Adds a ground fire patch that lingers for 3 seconds after activation, applying burn to enemies who walk through it.
- **Kindling**: Each burn tick has a 10% chance to refund a small amount of Sha-Chi.

With full investment, you can reduce the effective cooldown to roughly 8 seconds and the Sha-Chi cost to the point where you’re only sacrificing one heavy attack’s worth of resource per activation. This transforms the Flaming Mane from an occasional burst tool into a near-constant pressure option.

## Best Enemies to Use Fire Against

Not all enemies are created equal when it comes to elemental weaknesses. The Flaming Mane excels against specific enemy types:

### High-Priority Targets

- **Undead and Corpse-Type Enemies**: Take 30% bonus fire damage. The burn effect prevents certain undead from regenerating health.
- **Flesh Constructs**: Bosses stitched together from multiple bodies (seen in the Rainblood-connected lore) suffer amplified burn stacks.
- **Armored Soldiers**: As noted, burn bypasses armor entirely.
- **Grouped Infantry**: The cone shape punishes clustered enemies; one activation can tag an entire squad.

### Enemies to Avoid Using Fire On

- **Water-Attuned Enemies**: Some late-game enemies resist fire entirely.
- **Fire Elementals**: Obviously immune.
- **Fast, Teleporting Foes**: The cone has a brief wind-up animation; enemies that blink or dash can easily escape the area of effect.

## Combo Potential: Weaving Fire into Melee

The Flaming Mane’s true strength emerges when you treat it as a combo starter or extender rather than a standalone ability. Its moderate wind-up time means you should activate it during safe windows—but once you learn those windows, the payoff is enormous.

### Opening Combo (Safe Initiation)

1. Approach from mid-range.
2. Activate Flaming Mane to apply burn to the primary target and any nearby enemies.
3. As the flames animate, begin your dash-in attack (Ghostep works beautifully here).
4. Arrive as the burn ticks begin, pressuring the enemy’s block.
5. Use a heavy combo to break their guard into Breached state.
6. The amplified burn damage now shreds their health while you continue melee pressure.

### Punish Combo (Post-Parry)

1. Successfully parry a Brutal Move (gold/orange indicator).
2. The enemy staggers briefly.
3. Activate Flaming Mane point-blank—the entire cone hits a single target, potentially applying multiple burn stacks.
4. Follow with a charged heavy attack.
5. If they survive, the burn continues ticking during your reposition.

### Retreating Tech

If you’re overwhelmed, activate Flaming Mane while dodging backward. Enemies pursuing you run directly into the lingering ground fire (if you’ve taken Residual Embers), buying you precious seconds to heal or reposition.

## Weapon Synergies for Burn-Focused Builds

Not every weapon in Soul’s arsenal plays nicely with the Flaming Mane. The following pairings stand out:

### White Serpent & Crimson Viper (Dual Blades)

These rapid-strike dual blades apply hit-based status effects faster than any other weapon class. Pair them with the Flaming Mane, and you’re stacking burn from two sources simultaneously. The Viper specifically carries a native poison effect—enemies suffering both burn and poison melt at alarming speed.

### Soft Snake Sword

This whip-blade hybrid excels at mid-range harassment, exactly the distance where the Flaming Mane operates best. You can ignite enemies from safety, then lash them with the Snake Sword’s extended reach without ever entering their danger zone. Against bosses with wide melee sweeps, this combination keeps you comfortably outside their range while damage accumulates.

### Sanguine (Default Longsword)

Don’t overlook the starting weapon. Sanguine’s balanced moveset makes it the most reliable delivery system for burn-and-slash gameplay. Its straightforward combo paths mean you spend less brainpower on execution and more on timing your Flaming Mane activations. For players learning the burn playstyle, Sanguine provides a forgiving foundation.

### Weapons to Avoid

Heavy weapons like **Jagged Edge** or **Man-Cutter** consume enormous Sha-Chi on their own attacks. You’ll rarely have the spare resource to activate Flaming Mane mid-combo. If you’re committed to a heavy weapon build, consider a different Phantom Edge that costs less Sha-Chi.

## Building a Burn-Focused Playstyle

If you want to center your entire build around the Flaming Mane, here’s the blueprint:

### Essential Skill Tree Nodes

- **Phantom Efficiency I & II** (Sha-Chi cost reduction)
- **Lion’s Breath** (cooldown reduction)
- **Residual Embers** (ground fire)
- **Kindling** (Sha-Chi refund on burn ticks)
- **Scorched Earth** (burn duration +3 seconds)
- **Inferno** (max burn stacks increased from 3 to 4)

### Recommended Phantom Edge Pairings

You can equip multiple Phantom Edges and cycle between them. Consider these complementary tools:

- **Ice Shard Fan**: Apply slow after burn; enemies take more burn damage while slowed.
- **Poison Darts**: Double up on damage-over-time effects.
- **Concussive Bomb**: Group enemies together before hitting them with the Flaming Mane cone.

### Combat Loop

1. Initiate with Flaming Mane from range.
2. Dash in with Ghostep to close distance.
3. Build Sha-Chi with light attacks while burn ticks.
4. Spend Sha-Chi on heavy attacks to Breached the enemy.
5. Reactivate Flaming Mane (now off cooldown) for amplified burn during Breached state.
6. Finish with a Power Surge if available.

This loop maintains near-constant pressure. Even when you’re dodging or parrying, burn damage continues—and that’s the core philosophy of a burn build: *your damage never stops*.

## Flaming Mane in Boss Encounters

Certain boss fights showcase the Flaming Mane’s value more than others:

- **Coppermaul (Wan Jun)**: His slow, telegraphed swings give you ample windows to activate Flaming Mane. The burn damage helps offset his enormous health pool.
- **Chief Disciple of the Seven Stars**: Her second phase (Chief Disciple’s Fallen Hope) summons adds—the cone attack can tag her and her minions simultaneously.
- **Red Wraith**: This optional boss is aggressive but vulnerable to fire. Applying burn early means you’re still damaging him during his relentless attack strings when you’re forced to parry repeatedly.

## FAQ

**How much Sha-Chi does the Flaming Mane Mask cost to activate?**
The Flaming Mane Mask consumes approximately 15-20% of a full Sha-Chi bar at base. With Phantom Efficiency I and II from the Skill Tree, this can be reduced to roughly 10-12%, making it far more spammable in extended fights.

**Can burn stacks from the Flaming Mane kill an enemy without melee attacks?**
Yes. Burn damage ticks every second and can absolutely finish off an enemy without further melee input. This is particularly useful for finishing bosses during phases where you're forced to dodge or reposition—just let the flames do the work.

**Does the Flaming Mane work well against bosses, or is it better for mobs?**
It excels at both, but for different reasons. Against mobs, the cone shape tags multiple enemies at once. Against bosses, the persistent burn damage provides constant pressure even during defensive sequences. It's one of the most versatile Phantom Edges in the game.

**What's the best weapon to pair with a Flaming Mane burn build?**
The White Serpent & Crimson Viper dual blades are the strongest pairing, as their rapid strikes complement the damage-over-time playstyle and the Viper's native poison stacks multiplicatively with burn. For a more accessible option, the default Sanguine longsword offers a balanced, forgiving foundation.
